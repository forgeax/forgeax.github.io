做一部可玩的互动影游Make an interactive film-game

给一句灵感或一整部剧本,做出"限时点按 + 选择决定走向"的互动影游 —— AI 帮你拆剧情、定下角色与场景、生成分镜与视频,你来剪辑、试玩,最后一键打包成谁都能在浏览器里打开就玩的网页。From a single spark or a whole script, build an interactive film-game driven by timed taps and branching choices — AI breaks down the story, locks in characters and locations, generates storyboards and video; you edit, play-test, and ship it as a webpage anyone opens and plays in a browser.

✅ 已开源(Apache-2.0)。下面是创作流程的完整概览;克隆与安装见 文档。✅ Open source (Apache-2.0). Below is a full overview of the flow; for clone & install see the docs.

它是什么What it is

互动影游是"能玩的短片"。画面以视频和图片连续呈现,像看一部短剧;但在关键时刻,屏幕上会跳出限时点按——你得在几秒内做出反应;在一个场景结束时,又会给出选择,不同的选择把故事带向不同的下一幕,直到不同的结局。An interactive film-game is a short film you can play. Scenes flow as video and images, like watching a short drama — but at key moments a timed tap pops up, asking you to react within seconds; and at the end of a scene a choice branches the story toward a different next beat, all the way to different endings.

你在编辑器里把剧情、画面、节奏点和分支拼装好,玩家最终拿到的是一段确定性的、可反复游玩的体验:同样的选择走同样的路,换个选择就解锁新的剧情。You assemble the story, visuals, beats and branches in the editor; what players get is a deterministic, replayable experience — the same choices follow the same path, a different choice unlocks new story.

💡 一句话:你负责把故事讲清楚、把节奏和选择设计好;AI 负责把它"拍"出来、"剪"顺、变成能在浏览器里玩的成品。💡 In one line: you shape the story, the pacing and the choices; AI handles the "shooting", the "editing", and turning it into something that plays in a browser.

适合做什么What it's good for

  • 互动短剧 / 多结局故事 —— 让观众自己决定主角的下一步,一个故事玩出好几条线。Interactive shorts / multi-ending stories — let the audience decide the protagonist's next move; one story, several paths.
  • 剧情向小游戏 —— 把对话、悬念和限时反应做成轻量玩法,不需要写一行引擎代码。Story-driven mini games — turn dialogue, suspense and timed reactions into light gameplay, no engine code required.
  • 教学 / 演示 / 培训 —— 用"选了会怎样"的分支演练流程与决策,比纯视频更有参与感。Teaching / demos / training — drill processes and decisions with "what if I pick this" branches — more engaging than plain video.
  • 原型与试水 —— 几小时内做出可玩的雏形,先验证故事和玩法,再决定是否深做。Prototyping — build a playable rough cut in hours to validate story and feel before committing.

从剧本到成片,五步From script to film, in five steps

① 剧本:从一句灵感到结构化故事① Script: from a spark to a structured story

丢进一句灵感、一段梗概,或一整部剧本都行。AI 会把它扩写并结构化成分幕、角色、人物关系和逐场的详细剧情。故事太长也不怕——可以一集一集往下续,保持前后设定连贯。Drop in a one-line spark, a synopsis, or a whole script. AI expands and structures it into acts, characters, relationships and scene-by-scene beats. Long story? Continue it episode by episode while keeping earlier setups consistent.

② 锚点:先定"长什么样"② Anchors: lock the look first

系统自动从剧本里抽出角色、场景、关键道具(以及它们的不同状态与变体),让你先把"长什么样"定下来。之后所有画面都对齐这些锚点,所以人物不会换脸、场景不会跑偏——这是保持整部片一致性的关键一步。The system auto-extracts characters, locations and key props (and their states and variants) so you can lock their look up front. Every later shot aligns to these anchors, so characters don't swap faces and locations don't drift — the key step for keeping the whole film consistent.

③ 剧情树 · 分镜:把故事拆成一棵可玩的树③ Story tree · storyboards: split the story into a playable tree

AI 把剧情拆成一棵分镜树:每个节点是一个镜头,自动生成对应画面。你在树上自由编排——调整顺序与结构、加入选择分支(在某个节点之后岔出两条以上的路)、给紧张时刻设上限时点按。整棵树就是玩家会经历的所有可能路线。AI splits the story into a storyboard tree: each node is a shot with an auto-generated image. You arrange it freely — reorder and reshape, add branching choices (fork two or more paths after a node), and set timed taps on tense moments. The tree is every route a player can take.

④ 生成视频:让画面动起来④ Generate video: bring shots to life

分镜满意后,一键把镜头从静态画面生成视频。生成时可以带上参考图,让人物和场景在镜头里保持一致;台词可以配上选定的音色。所有素材统一落到时间轴上,和台词、节奏点对齐。Once the boards look right, turn shots from stills into video in one click. Reference images keep characters and locations consistent across shots; lines can be carried by a chosen voice. Everything lands on the timeline, aligned to dialogue and beats.

⑤ 剪辑 · 试玩:在时间轴上精修,边玩边改⑤ Edit · play-test: fine-tune on the timeline, tweak as you play

在三轨时间轴上精修:对齐台词、安排点按出现的时刻、连接分支去向。随时切到"试玩"全屏,像真正的玩家一样体验一遍——哪里节奏拖了、哪个点按太难、哪条分支不通顺,回来就改,改完再玩。Fine-tune on a three-track timeline: align dialogue, place when taps appear, wire where branches lead. Flip to a full-screen play-test anytime and experience it like a real player — wherever the pacing drags, a tap is too hard, or a branch feels off, jump back, fix it, and play again.

三条轨道:台词 / 点按 / 分支Three tracks: dialogue / taps / branches

影游的"可玩性"主要由时间轴上的三条轨道决定,它们各管一件事:A film-game's playability comes from three timeline tracks, each owning one job:

轨道Track 管什么What it controls 设计要点Design tip
台词Dialogue 每句话出现的时刻与时长,配上音色When each line shows and for how long, with a voice 让字幕和画面、语音对齐,别抢戏Keep subtitles in sync with picture and voice
点按Taps 限时反应:在窗口内点中算成功Timed reactions: tap within a window to succeed 放在紧张/动作的高点,窗口别太苛刻Place at tension peaks; don't make the window brutal
分支Branches 场景结尾给出选择,决定下一幕去向Choices at scene end that decide the next beat 两到三个选项最好,让每个选择都有后果Two or three options; make every choice matter

设计得更好玩的几个要点A few tips for better play

  • 把选择放在有分量的地方 —— 让玩家在"真的会改变故事"的节点上做决定,而不是无关紧要的二选一。Put choices where they count — ask players to decide at moments that truly change the story, not trivial forks.
  • 点按服务于情绪 —— 在动作、对峙、悬念这些高点放限时点按,强化"我在参与"的感觉。Taps serve emotion — drop timed taps on action, confrontation and suspense to amplify "I'm in it".
  • 分支收敛与发散并用 —— 不必每条线都全程独立,适当让几条线再汇回主线,既有变化又控制工作量。Mix diverging and converging — branches needn't stay fully separate; let some rejoin the main line to add variety while keeping scope sane.
  • 先短后长 —— 先做一条最短的完整线跑通体验,再往上加分支和结局。Short first, then long — get one shortest complete path working end-to-end, then layer on branches and endings.
  • 多试玩 —— 自己当玩家走一遍,往往比反复看时间轴更能发现问题。Play-test often — walking it as a player usually surfaces issues faster than staring at the timeline.

打包 · 分享Package & share

做好的影游可以一键打包成一个自包含的网页:剧情、图片、视频、点按与分支逻辑全都打进去。把它发给别人,在任意现代浏览器里打开就能玩——不用安装任何东西,也不挑显卡。A finished film-game packages into one self-contained webpage: story, images, video, taps and branch logic all bundled in. Send it to anyone and it plays in any modern browser — no install, no GPU needed.

在工程里,它还会作为一份标准游戏资产被保存与寻址,因此可以被复用、被引用,也方便再分发或接入更大的项目。Inside a project it's also saved and addressed as a standard game asset, so it can be reused, referenced, redistributed, or wired into a larger project.

常见问题FAQ

  • 玩家需要装东西吗? 不需要。打包出来就是一个网页,双击或托管后用浏览器打开即可。Do players need to install anything? No. The package is a webpage — open it in a browser, locally or hosted.
  • 我不会拍片、不会剪辑,能用吗? 能。你只要把故事和选择讲清楚,生成与对齐都交给 AI 和时间轴。I can't shoot or edit — can I still use it? Yes. Just shape the story and the choices; generation and alignment are handled by AI and the timeline.
  • 故事很长可以吗? 可以,按集续写,每集都对齐已定下的角色与场景锚点。What about long stories? Fine — continue episode by episode, each aligned to the characters and locations you've already locked.
  • 之后还能改吗? 能。它在工程里是可寻址的资产,随时回到编辑器调整后重新打包。Can I edit later? Yes. It's an addressable asset in your project — return to the editor, adjust, and repackage anytime.
