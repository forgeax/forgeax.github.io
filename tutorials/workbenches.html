创作Create概览Overview

用工作台生成美术资产Generate art with the workbenches

除了对话,ForgeaX 还有一整套"工作台" —— 每个工作台是一个面板 + 一组工具,把一句话变成可进游戏的素材。你手点和 AI 派活,改的是同一份产物。Beyond chatting, ForgeaX has a set of "workbenches" — each a panel plus a group of tools that turn a sentence into game-ready assets. You by hand and AI by dispatch edit the same artifact.

✅ 已开源(Apache-2.0)。下面是工作台体系的概览;克隆与安装见 文档。✅ Open source (Apache-2.0). Below is an overview of the workbench system; for clone & install see the docs.

角色 · 动画 · 特效Characters · animation · VFX

  • 角色编辑器 —— 写一句人物设定,得到正面立绘 + 转身三视图。Character editor — write a one-line brief, get a portrait plus a turnaround.
  • 动画 —— 把静止角色变成会走、会打、四方向、带骨骼动作,甚至短视频;不串脸。Animation — turn a still character into walking, attacking, four-direction, skeletal motion (even short clips) — without face drift.
  • 技能 / VFX —— 给技能配火光、刀光、光环等特效,自动挂到出手那一刻。Skill / VFX — add effects like fire, slashes and auras, auto-attached to the moment of cast.

3D 建模与生成3D modeling & generation

  • 方块人编辑器 —— 像捏积木小人一样做可动 3D 角色,一键导出标准 3D 文件。Blocky-character editor — build rigged 3D characters like snapping blocks, export standard 3D files.
  • 低多边形生成器 —— 用"连线搭积木"的节点方式做 3D 道具 / 机械,改一个参数全套重出。Low-poly generator — build 3D props with a node-graph; change one parameter, regenerate the set.
  • 3D 生成 —— 文字 / 图片直接变 3D 模型,接入多家最强生成服务,择优而用。3D generation — text or image straight to a 3D model, plugged into several top generators, pick whichever's best.

场景 · UI · 道具Scenes · UI · items

  • 场景生成器 —— 自动"搭"出一整张游戏场景,做成流程图,想改随时重跑。Scene generator — assemble a whole game scene as a graph you can re-run anytime.
  • 2D 场景资产生成器 —— 批量生成道具、贴图、装饰、UI 小图,自动起名归档。2D scene-asset generator — batch props, textures, decorations and UI bits, auto-named and filed.
  • UI 工坊 —— 按你的游戏类型出一套界面,不只是图,是能点的交互原型。UI workbench — a UI set for your game type — not just images, a clickable prototype.
  • 道具 / 图标 —— 做武器、药水、金币等图标,还配上数据,图与数据一张表。Items / icons — weapons, potions, coins as icons with their data, art and numbers in one table.

叙事 · 音乐 · 调色 · 数值Narrative · music · look · numbers

  • 叙事工坊 —— 从一句灵感写出整套剧情和世界观,AI 自动选最合适的写法。Narrative workbench — from a spark to a full story and world, with the AI picking a fitting approach.
  • 音乐 / BGM —— 管理背景音乐与音效,排好整局的配乐节奏。Music / BGM — manage background music and sound effects, sequencing a whole session's score.
  • 色彩 / Look —— 一键统一全游戏的色调氛围,像给画面套滤镜。Color / Look — unify the whole game's color mood in one click, like a filter over everything.
  • 平衡 / 数值 —— 让 AI 反复打几千局,给出胜率与置信区间,数值好不好用数据说话。Balance — have AI play thousands of matches and report win rates with confidence intervals; let data judge the tuning.

系统与作者工具System & author tools

  • 代码 —— 在工作台里看代码、跳转文件,和写代码的 AI 一起改。Code — browse code, jump between files, and edit alongside the coding AI.
  • 轨迹观察台 —— 像黑匣子一样回放 AI 每一步,精确定位是哪一步出的问题。Observatory — replay every step the AI took, like a black box, to pinpoint where things went wrong.
  • 插件作者 —— 让不会编程的人也能做出自己的工作台插件。Plugin author — let non-coders build their own workbench plugins.
  • 面板管理 —— 工作室总控台:开关工作台、绑模型、看各家 AI 是否在线。Admin — the studio control panel: toggle workbenches, bind models, see which AIs are online.
不会画画、不会建模、不会作曲也没关系 —— 描述出来,工作台帮你做出能直接进游戏的素材。Can't draw, model or compose? That's fine — describe it, and the workbenches produce assets that drop straight into your game.
