v0.3.22 — Play-Modus-Fix v2, Spielwechsel-Fix v2, Editor-Panel-Beiträge, Video-Assets

Play-Modus-Sichtbarkeitspause mit dynamischer aktiver App-Auflösung verbessert, Weißbildschirm beim Spielwechsel vollständig behoben, Editor-Panel-Beiträge und vereinheitlichte Video-Asset-Verwaltung hinzugefügt.

Play mode freeze fix (enhanced)

  • installVisibilityPause now dynamically resolves the active app (editorApp vs playApp) when the viewport is hidden/shown. Switching from Scene (Play mode) to the AI view and back correctly pauses and resumes the play app's frame loop instead of the editor app.

Game creation blank screen fix (enhanced)

  • setPinnedSlug is now deferred until after activateGame + waitForEngineSettled, preventing EditRealm from remounting the viewport while the engine Vite server is still restarting. Eliminates the 502 race condition on pack-index and asset fetches.

Editor panel contributions

  • Plugins can now register custom editor panels via the panel contributions extension API.

Video asset management

  • Unified video asset management across the editor, with integrated upload, preview, and timeline editing.

Activity Rail and chat

  • Activity Rail now supports plugin navigation entries. Chat panel is resizable.

Hellforge vertical slice

  • Landed the full vertical-slice polish plan (PR0–PR7) for the Hellforge demo game, covering quality camera, dodge/finisher, VFX data model, atmosphere/grade, modular seeded dungeon, cinematic ownership, and production audio.
