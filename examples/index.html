Engine Examples

Pick a demo — it runs live on the right. Basics fold into Fundamentals. Drag 3D scenes to orbit; scroll to zoom.

glTF Instancing Four lit cubes, one mesh, one draw — GPU instancing from a glTF file. Clustered Lighting 256 punctual lights lit efficiently with a clustered-forward pipeline. Physics Rigid-body physics via Rapier (wasm) driving ECS transforms. Sprites 2D sprite rendering — batched quads with layer-z and JRPG Y-sort. 2D Flashlight Sprite-lit 2D scene — a sweeping spot light and a soft point light. Sprite Lighting Four sprite-lit quads plus a PBR cube under the same directional, point and spot lights. Room A small interior — box, sphere and plane composed as a nested scene.
