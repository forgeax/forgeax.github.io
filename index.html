你的个人游戏工作室。Your personal game studio.

ForgeaX 是一个 AI 原生的游戏开发工作室 —— 把你的想法变成可玩的游戏，AI 替你编写引擎代码、实时热重载跑在浏览器里。更不寻常的是：连这套引擎与平台本身，也几乎完全由 AI 在一个自主开发循环里一行行写成；它一天天自建自己的过程，就记在更新日志里，像一份 AI 的长期开发记忆。 ForgeaX is an AI-native game development studio — turn your ideas into playable games while AI writes the engine code, hot-reloading live in your browser. More unusually: the engine and platform themselves are written almost entirely by AI, line by line, in a self-running development loop — and its day-by-day self-build is logged in the changelog, a kind of long-term development memory for the AI.
