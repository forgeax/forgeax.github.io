每个新建游戏开箱就有真实天光;商店新增"文字生成 3D 模型"工作台;场景定义收敛到一处,编辑与试玩更一致。Every new game ships with a real sky; a new text-to-3D model workbench arrives; scene definitions converge so edit and play stay in sync.

所有游戏共用一张天空盒,新建即有真天光All games share one sky — new ones look right out of the box

环境天空被抽到一处共享资源,每个游戏在打包时折叠引用、并把高动态范围(HDR)环境贴图一次烤好,运行时直接读用。结果就是:新建一个游戏开箱就带真实的天光氛围,而不是一片灰色渐变;编辑视图也接上了同一套天空,编辑器里看到的环境光和试玩时一致,也不再有缺资源的报错。新游戏的"第一眼"一下子像样多了。The environment sky was pulled into one shared resource: each game folds in a reference at build time and the high-dynamic-range (HDR) environment map is baked once, read directly at runtime. The result: a newly created game ships with real sky atmosphere out of the box instead of a flat gray gradient; the edit view uses the same sky, so the ambient light you see in the editor matches play, with no more missing-asset errors. A new game's "first impression" is suddenly much more presentable.

商店新增"文字生成 3D 模型"工作台A new text-to-3D model workbench in the store

工具家族新增一款 3D 生成工作台:用文字描述就能生成 3D 模型。它接入了多家文生 3D 的生成服务可供选择,带姿态预处理,生成结果会落盘持久化;还内置了模型预览和图片预览,生成完直接在工作台里转着看。整个工作台同源嵌入在 Studio 页面里打开,生成的模型和图片可以直接取用、接进你的游戏。"想要个石头/箱子/小怪模型"从此可以一句话生成。The tool family gained a 3D-generation workbench: describe something in words and get a 3D model. It connects to several text-to-3D generation services to choose from, includes pose preprocessing, and persists results to disk; it also has built-in model and image previews, so you can spin a result around right in the workbench. The whole workbench embeds same-origin inside the Studio page, and the generated models and images can be used directly and wired into your game. "I want a rock / crate / little-monster model" can now be a single sentence.

场景定义收敛到一处:编辑与试玩更一致Scene definitions converge: edit and play stay in sync

原先"场景"这层数据在编辑器、游戏、引擎里各有一份独立来源,调试时编辑态和试玩态经常对不上。这天把它收敛成单一来源,一个"场景对象"只有一处定义,并给编辑→试玩的状态同步加了双保险——你在编辑器里摆好的东西,切到试玩时如实呈现。引擎侧还支持了场景嵌套(一个场景里可以套用另一个场景),以及把大型多材质场景完整加载,组合复杂关卡更方便。The "scene" layer of data used to have separate sources in the editor, the games and the engine, so edit and play often disagreed during debugging. This day converged it to a single source — one "scene object" has one definition — and added a double safeguard to edit-to-play sync, so what you arrange in the editor shows up faithfully when you switch to play. The engine side also supports scene nesting (one scene can embed another) and full loading of large multi-material scenes, making complex levels easier to compose.
