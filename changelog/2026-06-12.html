角色真的动起来了:骨骼动画整条链跑通Characters actually move: the skeletal-animation chain runs end to end

这是一个里程碑式的画面:一个带骨骼的角色模型,现在能真正播放它自带的动画——走、跑、跳的每一帧,都由动画系统驱动骨架、再带动表面网格变形,而不再是僵在原地的一尊雕像。技术上,这一步打通了骨骼动画最核心的环节:每一帧,系统会算出每根骨头当前在世界里的姿态,再乘上一个"绑定时的初始姿态"的逆变换,得到一组描述"每根骨头相对初始位置偏移了多少"的矩阵,把这组矩阵一次性传给显卡;顶点着色器读到它,就能按"每个顶点受哪几根骨头影响、各影响多少"把网格表面正确地变形——这套方法就是角色动画的标准做法。它建立在前一天打通的顶点数据链路和多格式模型导入之上,几天的铺垫到这天终于开花结果。现在示例里能让多个带骨骼的角色同时做动作。对一个以做游戏为目标的引擎来说,"角色会动"几乎是一切的前提——它一旦成立,后面的战斗、互动、表演才都有了根。A milestone moment: a rigged character model can now genuinely play its built-in animation — every frame of walking, running and jumping is driven by the animation system moving the skeleton, which in turn deforms the surface mesh, rather than a statue frozen in place. Technically, this completed the most core link of skeletal animation: each frame, the system computes every bone's current world pose, multiplies it by the inverse of its "initial pose at binding time" to get a set of matrices describing "how much each bone has shifted from its initial position," and uploads that set to the GPU all at once; the vertex shader reads it and deforms the mesh surface correctly by "which bones affect each vertex, and by how much" — this method is the standard way character animation is done. It builds on the previous day's vertex-data chain and multi-format model import, and several days of groundwork finally bore fruit this day. The samples can now have several rigged characters performing actions at once. For an engine aiming to make games, "characters move" is almost the precondition for everything — once it holds, combat, interaction and performance later all have a root.

生成 → 绑骨 → 加动作:一条角色产线成形Generate → rig → animate: a character pipeline takes shape

和引擎让角色动起来相呼应,角色生成工作台这天推进到了一个关键里程碑:能给生成出来的模型自动绑骨、加动作,并配上一条减面产线。这几件事各自解决一个具体环节。"绑骨"是给一个静态的模型装上一副可驱动的骨架——只有装了骨架,模型才谈得上动;这一步传统上需要美术手工完成,既费时又需要专业技能。"加动作"是把标准的动作(走、跑、待机等)映射到这副骨架上,让角色一生成出来就自带一套基本动作。"减面"则是把生成出来往往面数过高、运行起来很重的模型,自动简化成游戏里跑得动的低模,同时尽量保留外观。把这三步和前面的"文字 / 图生成模型"接起来,就形成了一条完整的角色产线:你描述一个角色 → 它被生成出来 → 自动绑好骨架 → 套上动作 → 优化成能用的低模,最后直接是一个能放进游戏里走来走去的活角色。对没有美术和动画功底的人来说,这条产线把"做一个会动的角色"这件原本门槛极高的事,压缩成了几步自动化流程。Echoing the engine making characters move, the character-generation workbench reached a key milestone this day: automatically rigging generated models, adding motion, and providing a polygon-reduction pipeline. Each addresses a concrete stage. "Rigging" fits a static model with a drivable skeleton — only with a skeleton can a model move at all; traditionally this needs an artist by hand, costly in time and skill. "Adding motion" maps standard actions (walk, run, idle and so on) onto that skeleton, so a character comes with a basic action set the moment it's generated. "Polygon reduction" automatically simplifies the often too-high-poly, runtime-heavy generated model into a game-runnable low-poly version while preserving its look as much as possible. Connecting these three to the earlier "text/image-to-model" forms a complete character pipeline: you describe a character → it's generated → automatically rigged → fitted with motion → optimized into a usable low-poly model, ending as a living character you can drop into a game and have it walk around. For people without art or animation skills, this pipeline compresses "make a moving character" — once an extremely high bar — into a few automated steps.

聊天更稳:专项修内存泄漏,挂一整天也不卡A steadier chat: a focused leak fix, so it won't bog down all day

聊天这天做了一轮针对稳定性的专项,核心是集中修掉了几处内存泄漏。所谓内存泄漏,就是程序用过的内存没被及时回收,越积越多,时间一长就把界面拖得越来越卡——对一个你可能要开着聊一整天的对话面板来说,这是最影响长时体验的隐患。这天从几个源头一一堵住:超长的对话历史会被限量保留,不再无限制地堆在内存里;向上翻看旧消息时改成分页加载,避免一次性把成千上万条消息全塞进页面把它撑垮;每条消息曾经会悄悄注册一个窗口缩放的监听器却不释放,越积越多,这个泄漏也被修掉了。这些问题平时不显眼,只在"用得久"时才暴露,但恰恰是它们决定了一个工具能不能被信任地长时间使用。把这些隐形的消耗一处处堵住,等于让聊天面板从"用一会儿挺好、用久了发卡"变成了"开一整天也稳"。Chat got a stability-focused pass this day, centered on fixing several memory leaks. A memory leak is when memory a program has used isn't reclaimed in time and keeps piling up, dragging the interface slower over time — for a conversation panel you might keep open all day, this is the biggest hidden threat to the long-session experience. This day plugged it at several sources one by one: very long conversation history is now capped rather than piling up in memory without limit; scrolling up to old messages pages them in instead of stuffing thousands into the page at once and bloating it; each message used to quietly register a window-resize listener without releasing it, accumulating endlessly, and this leak was fixed too. These problems are inconspicuous in normal use and only surface with "long use," but they're exactly what decides whether a tool can be trusted for extended sessions. Plugging these invisible drains one by one turns the chat panel from "fine for a while, laggy after long use" into "stable even open all day."

流式时也能打字 + AI 能向你提结构化的问题Type while streaming + the AI can ask you structured questions

聊天的交互这天也补了两处很实用的能力。一是"流式期间可输入":以前模型还在一行行往外吐字时,输入框往往是锁住的,你得干等它说完才能打字;现在你可以一边看它输出、一边把下一条想说的话敲好,排进队列随时发出去,不必中断思路去等。二是 AI 能向你提"结构化的问题":当 agent 需要你做个选择或确认时,它不再把问题混在一长段文字里让你费力辨认,而是直接弹出一张"询问卡片"——上面列出可点选的选项(单选或多选),也允许你填自定义答案。这件事的意义不只是好看:它把"人机之间需要拍板的关键决策"变成了一次清晰、明确、不会误读的交互。对一个 AI 主导、但人随时要介入做决定的协作模式来说,"AI 怎么把问题清楚地抛给人、人怎么干脆地回答"是个核心环节。把它做成规整的卡片,等于给这种关键时刻的对话装上了一个可靠的接口。Chat's interaction also gained two very practical abilities this day. One is "input during streaming": previously, while the model was still spilling text line by line, the input box was often locked and you had to wait for it to finish before typing; now you can watch it output while typing your next message, queue it and send it whenever, without interrupting your train of thought to wait. The other is the AI asking you "structured questions": when an agent needs you to choose or confirm, it no longer mixes the question into a long paragraph you must labor to parse, but pops an "ask card" — listing clickable options (single or multi-select) and also allowing a custom answer. Its significance is more than looks: it turns "the key decisions humans must make in the loop" into a clear, explicit, unmisreadable interaction. For a collaboration model that's AI-led but where humans must step in to decide at any time, "how the AI cleanly hands a question to a person, and how the person crisply answers" is a core link. Making it a tidy card equips the conversation at these key moments with a reliable interface.

编辑器能独立运行 + 跨界面消息严格校验The editor can run standalone + strictly validated cross-surface messages

这天还在架构上做了一件偏长远的事:把前端外壳抽成了一套可复用的应用框架,并提供了几个公开接口,于是编辑器可以脱离主界面、作为一个独立应用单独挂载运行。这让编辑器从"只能长在主 Studio 里的一块"变成了"一个能被单独打开、被嵌入别处的模块"——更模块化,也为后面把它装进桌面应用、嵌入其他页面等场景留好了空间。与此配套,底层把界面各部分之间、以及跨内嵌页面之间传递的消息全部加上了双侧严格校验:每一条消息在发出和接收两端都按预先定义好的结构核对一遍,格式不对就直接拦下不放行。这件事看似琐碎,实则关键——跨界面、跨内嵌页面的状态同步,过去是各种诡异 bug 的高发区:一边以为发对了、另一边收到的却是个对不上的东西,排查起来极其耗神。给每条消息都立一份明确的"格式契约"并双侧校验,等于从根上杜绝了"瞎传一气、对不上号"的隐患,让跨界面协作变得可预期、可信赖。This day also did something more long-term on the architecture: extracting the front-end shell into a reusable application framework with a few public interfaces, so the editor can detach from the main interface and mount and run as a standalone app. That turns the editor from "a piece that can only live inside the main Studio" into "a module that can be opened on its own and embedded elsewhere" — more modular, and leaving room for later scenarios like packing it into a desktop app or embedding it in other pages. Alongside, messages passed between parts of the interface and across embedded pages now all carry strict two-sided validation: each message is checked against a predefined structure at both the sending and receiving ends, and rejected if malformed. It seems trivial but is key — cross-surface, cross-embedded-page state sync used to be a hotbed of weird bugs: one side thinks it sent correctly while the other receives something that doesn't match, agonizing to debug. Giving each message a clear "format contract" validated on both sides eliminates the "blind passing, mismatched" hazard at the root, making cross-surface collaboration predictable and trustworthy.

监控台多了一个可翻阅的知识库The monitoring console gains a browsable knowledge base

这天给监控台加了一个完整的"知识库"标签页,可以在里面翻阅沉淀下来的资料与条目。它的左侧是一个分面筛选边栏,把条目按类型、标签、领域分组并可折叠;右侧是双列的条目网格,点开任意一条会弹出预览;顶部还有一组可点选的标签云。配套有一连串细节打磨——刷新有动画反馈、滚动条用主题色细条、分组标题可折叠等等,让翻阅体验顺手。为什么一个开发平台需要一个内建的知识库?因为当大量工作由 AI 推进时,沉淀下来的经验、规范、决策记录就成了团队和 AI 共同的"记忆";把这些知识结构化地组织起来、能被随时检索翻阅,既方便人查阅,也是 AI 做事有据可依的基础。同一天还把指导整个项目的设计原则重新梳理成了"几条压缩公理加两条边界"的形式——核心信念始终如一:衡量系统好坏的不是代码多少,而是理解它需要同时记住多少个概念,概念越少越好。把知识可检索、把原则可表述,都是同一种追求:让这套日益复杂的系统,对人和 AI 都始终保持可理解。This day added a complete "knowledge base" tab to the monitoring console, where you can browse accumulated material and entries. Its left side is a faceted filter sidebar grouping entries by type, tags and domain, collapsible; the right side is a two-column entry grid, and opening any entry pops a preview; the top has a clickable tag cloud. A string of detail polish came with it — animated refresh feedback, theme-colored thin scrollbars, collapsible group headers and so on — making browsing smooth. Why does a development platform need a built-in knowledge base? Because when much work is pushed by AI, the accumulated experience, conventions and decision records become the shared "memory" of the team and the AI; organizing this knowledge in a structured, searchable, browsable way helps people look things up and gives the AI a basis to act on. The same day, the design principles guiding the whole project were reorganized into the form of "a few compression axioms plus two boundaries" — the core belief unchanged: a system's quality is measured not by how much code there is but by how many concepts you must hold to understand it, fewer being better. Making knowledge searchable and principles expressible are the same pursuit: keeping this ever-more-complex system understandable to both people and AI.
