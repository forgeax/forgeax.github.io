这天同一个工程能放多个关卡了,还配了关卡启动器;引擎阴影系统一次升档——大场景级联阴影 + 点光源阴影 + 完整的 HDR 与辉光;弹幕生存样例被做成了"白天牧场 + 夜晚墓地"的双关卡战役,顺带把整套粒子特效捋顺;引擎和聊天两端同时围猎资源泄漏;agent 的可靠性也做了一轮加固。This day one project can hold multiple levels, with a level launcher; the engine's shadow system stepped up at once — cascaded shadows for large scenes, point-light shadows, and complete HDR and bloom; the bullet-survival sample became a two-level "day pasture + night graveyard" campaign while its whole particle-effects chain was straightened out; leaks were hunted on both the engine and the chat side; and agent reliability got a hardening pass.

一边做大,一边收紧Growing bigger while tightening up

这一天的工作可以分成"做大"和"收紧"两股劲。做大的一面:工程能装下多个关卡,意味着创作的单位从"一个场景"升级成了"一整套游戏";引擎的阴影、HDR、辉光一次升档,意味着画面能往真实、有质感的方向再推一大步;弹幕生存样例被做成完整的双关卡战役,则把这些新能力当场演示了一遍。收紧的一面:引擎和聊天两端都在围猎"资源泄漏"这个隐形敌人,把用完不还的显存和内存一处处堵上;agent 的可靠性也做了专项加固,让它在出错时能自己重试、扛得住超长上下文。一个平台在快速长大的同时,必须同步把根基收紧,否则规模越大、漏洞越多。这一天恰好把这两件事放在了一起做。The day's work splits into two forces: "growing bigger" and "tightening up." On the growing side: a project can hold multiple levels, lifting the unit of creation from "one scene" to "a whole set of a game"; the engine's shadows, HDR and bloom stepped up at once, meaning visuals can push another big step toward realism and texture; and the bullet-survival sample became a complete two-level campaign, demonstrating these new capabilities on the spot. On the tightening side: both the engine and the chat hunted the invisible enemy of "resource leaks," plugging spot by spot the video memory and memory that weren't returned after use; agent reliability also got a focused hardening so it can retry on error and withstand very long context. A platform growing fast must tighten its foundation in step, or the bigger the scale, the more the holes. This day happened to do both at once.

一个工程能放多个关卡 + 关卡启动器Multiple levels in one project + a level launcher

这天编辑器接入了"多场景游戏",这是一个把创作单位放大的关键变化。此前一个工程基本对应一个场景,想做有多关、有分支的完整游戏就很别扭;现在同一个工程下可以并存多个关卡文件,资产面板也改成按名称组织"子资产",不再把同一个场景拆成一行行重复堆在列表里看着眼花。配套新增了一个关卡启动器面板,让你在 Studio 里直接挑选要进哪一关来编辑或试玩;编辑态和试玩态之间会成对同步,你在编辑器里改了什么,切到试玩就如实呈现。为了把这套能力坐实,配套的弹幕生存样例也被做成了一个真正的多关卡战役——一关是白天的牧场、一关是夜晚的墓地,两种完全不同的氛围,正是"用一个工程做出一整套关卡"的实战示范。从这天起,做一个有起承转合、有多个关卡的完整游戏,在结构上终于顺理成章,而不再需要为每一关单开一个工程、再手动拼起来。This day the editor gained "multi-scene games," a key change that enlarges the unit of creation. Previously a project basically mapped to one scene, making a complete game with multiple branching levels awkward; now multiple level files can coexist in one project, and the asset panel organizes "sub-assets" by name rather than stacking the same scene into dizzying duplicate rows. A level launcher panel was added so you can pick which level to enter for editing or play right in Studio; edit and play stay paired in sync, so whatever you change in the editor shows up faithfully on switching to play. To make this capability concrete, the bullet-survival sample became a real multi-level campaign — one level a daytime pasture, one a night graveyard, two entirely different moods — a hands-on demonstration of "build a whole set of levels in one project." From this day, building a complete game with a beginning-middle-end and multiple levels is finally structurally natural, no longer needing a separate project per level manually stitched together.

引擎阴影系统升档:近清晰、远平滑、点光也投影A shadow upgrade: crisp near, smooth far, point lights cast too

引擎的阴影这天一次升了一档,而阴影恰恰是决定一个三维画面"可不可信"的关键——没有阴影,物体就像浮在空中、贴在背景上,毫无立体感。核心的一项是新增了"级联阴影"并把它定为统一的阴影方案。它解决的是大场景阴影的老大难:一张大地图,如果用一张固定精度的阴影图去覆盖,要么近处糊、要么远处糊;级联阴影的思路是把视野按远近分成几段,近处用高精度、远处用低精度,于是脚边的阴影清晰锐利、远处的阴影平滑过渡,整张大地图都经得起看。另一项是点光源现在也能投影了——以前只有像太阳那样的平行光能产生阴影,而一盏灯泡、一支火把这种向四面八方发光的点光源照不出影子;现在它们也能投出正确的、向四周发散的阴影。再配上完整的 HDR(高动态范围,让画面明暗层次更真实)与辉光范例,画面的真实感整体抬升。这些是后续做出有质感的真实场景必不可少的图形地基。The engine's shadows stepped up a level this day, and shadows are exactly what decides whether a 3D image is "believable" — without them, objects seem to float in air or stick to the background, with no sense of solidity. A core addition was "cascaded shadows," set as the unified approach. It solves the perennial problem of large-scene shadows: covering a big map with one fixed-resolution shadow map blurs either the near or the far; cascaded shadows split the view into segments by distance, high resolution up close and lower far away, so shadows at your feet are crisp and sharp while distant ones transition smoothly, and the whole big map holds up. The other addition is that point lights can now cast shadows — previously only sun-like directional light produced shadows, while a bulb or a torch radiating in all directions cast none; now they too throw correct shadows spreading all around. With complete HDR (high dynamic range, for more realistic light-and-dark gradation) and bloom examples, the overall realism rose. These are the indispensable graphics foundation for building believable real scenes from here.

弹幕生存样例:从"能跑"到"像个真游戏"The bullet-survival sample: from "it runs" to "looks like a real game"

围绕那款弹幕生存样例,这天把整条粒子与特效链路彻底捋顺了一遍,顺带把它从"能跑"打磨成了"看着像个真游戏"。具体做了一连串事:把粒子系统改成实例化的方式,于是满屏的火花、爆炸、光效同时出现也不卡顿;修对了加色混合与透明渲染的处理,消除了之前那种特效边缘出现的发黑方块;把冲击波从一团杂乱的效果改成了干净利落的薄环;给每种技能配了各自的自定义着色器,让爆炸、火焰、冰冻各有各的质感;再叠上一层电影感的画面后处理,以及给怪物和玩家角色做的整套外观资产。这件事的价值是双重的:一方面,它让这个样例本身变成了一个观感不错、可以直接玩也可以照着学的成品;另一方面,它实际上是用一个真游戏,把当天引擎刚升级的阴影、辉光、粒子、特效等一整套图形能力,完完整整地跑了一遍压力测试——能把它撑起来,才证明这些底层能力是真的好用。Around that bullet-survival sample, this day thoroughly straightened out the whole particle-and-effects chain, polishing it from "it runs" into "looks like a real game." A string of concrete work: the particle system was changed to an instanced approach, so sparks, explosions and light effects filling the screen at once no longer stutter; additive blending and transparent rendering were fixed, eliminating the blackened squares that used to appear at effect edges; shockwaves went from a messy blob into clean thin rings; each skill got its own custom shader, giving explosion, fire and ice their own feel; topped with a layer of cinematic post-processing, and a full set of appearance assets for the monsters and player character. The value is twofold: on one hand, the sample itself became a good-looking finished product you can play directly or learn from; on the other, it actually stress-tested, end to end, the whole set of graphics capabilities the engine had just upgraded that day — shadows, bloom, particles, effects — with a real game. Only by holding it up is it proven that these underlying capabilities are genuinely usable.

两端同时围猎资源泄漏Hunting resource leaks on both ends

这天有一条贯穿前后端的暗线:围猎"资源泄漏"。所谓泄漏,就是用过的资源没被及时归还,越积越多,最终拖垮整个系统——它平时不显眼,只在长时间、大规模使用时集中爆发,因而格外阴险。引擎这一侧,补上了显卡资源的完整生命周期管理:每一块显存缓冲、每一张纹理在用完后都能被明确地销毁回收,渲染器整体也能干净地释放——以前这些显存可能悄悄漏着,跑久了显存越占越满。聊天这一侧,则一口气清掉了好几处前端状态的内存泄漏:关闭一个会话时,与它相关的各种缓存、监听、临时记录都会被正确地清除,而不是残留在内存里越堆越多。这两件事看起来一个在底层渲染、一个在前端界面,毫不相干,但内核是同一条原则:谁申请的资源,谁负责在用完后归还。把这条原则在系统的每一个角落都贯彻到位,正是一个软件能够长时间、大体量稳定运行的根本保证。This day had a hidden thread running through front and back: hunting "resource leaks." A leak is resources used but not returned in time, piling up until they drag the whole system down — inconspicuous in normal use, erupting only under long, large-scale use, and thus especially insidious. On the engine side, complete lifecycle management for GPU resources was added: every video-memory buffer and texture can be explicitly destroyed and reclaimed after use, and the renderer as a whole can release cleanly — previously this video memory might quietly leak, filling up over a long run. On the chat side, several front-end state memory leaks were cleared at once: when a session is closed, the various caches, listeners and temporary records tied to it are correctly evicted rather than lingering in memory and piling up. The two seem unrelated — one in low-level rendering, one in the front-end interface — but the core is the same principle: whoever requests a resource is responsible for returning it after use. Enforcing this principle in every corner of the system is the fundamental guarantee that software can run stably for a long time at large scale.

Agent 可靠性加固:出错能自己重试、扛得住长上下文Agent-reliability hardening: it can retry on error and withstand long context

这天还给 agent 的可靠性做了一轮针对性加固,专攻几类最影响"AI 干活靠不靠谱"的问题。其一,是在 agent 的工作循环内部加了对大模型调用的重试:模型调用偶尔会因为网络抖动等原因失败,以前一次失败可能就让整个任务中断,现在它会在循环里自己再试一次,把这种偶发故障消化掉。其二,是当对话上下文太长、快要超出模型容量时,系统会主动地、有反应地把历史压缩一下,避免直接撞上限而崩掉。其三,是加固了把任务派给子 agent 时的回调处理,以及对事件积压的报告。这些改动还配了一套专门的验证手段——用一个可脚本控制的"假模型服务器"来模拟各种异常情况,在真实的运行栈里把这些加固逐一验证过。一个让 AI 替你长时间自主干活的平台,最怕的就是它在中途因为某个偶发问题悄无声息地卡住或崩掉;把这些薄弱环节一个个补强,正是让人敢于"放手让它跑"的底气所在。This day also gave agent reliability a targeted hardening pass, focusing on the classes of problems that most affect "whether the AI is dependable at work." First, retry of large-model calls was added inside the agent's work loop: a model call occasionally fails due to network jitter and the like, and where one failure used to break the whole task, it now retries itself within the loop, absorbing such sporadic faults. Second, when the conversation context grows too long and nears the model's capacity, the system proactively and reactively compresses the history, avoiding hitting the limit and crashing. Third, the callback handling when delegating a task to a sub-agent was hardened, along with reporting of event backlog. These changes came with a dedicated verification means — a scriptable "fake model server" simulating various abnormal situations, validating each hardening in a real running stack. A platform that has the AI work autonomously for you over long stretches most fears it quietly stalling or crashing midway on some sporadic problem; reinforcing these weak links one by one is exactly the confidence that lets people dare to "let it run."

这一天意味着什么What this day means

这一天把"野心"和"底气"摆在了一起。多关卡工程和阴影升档,代表的是野心——它让平台能承载更完整、更宏大、更好看的作品,创作的天花板被又一次抬高;而资源泄漏的围猎与 agent 可靠性的加固,代表的是底气——它让这些更大的野心能稳稳地落在一个不漏、不崩、出错能自愈的根基上。一个只追野心不顾底气的产品,做得越大、塌得越惨;一个只守底气不敢追野心的产品,则永远停在原地。真正成熟的工程节奏,是让这两者像这一天一样彼此配速:每往前迈一大步能力,就同时往下夯一层根基。那个被做成完整双关卡战役、画面精致的样例,正是这种节奏最好的注脚——它既展示了能做到多好,也用自己跑通了这一切是否真的稳。This day placed "ambition" and "assurance" side by side. Multi-level projects and the shadow upgrade represent ambition — letting the platform carry more complete, grander, better-looking works, raising the creative ceiling once more; while the leak hunt and agent-reliability hardening represent assurance — letting these bigger ambitions rest steadily on a foundation that doesn't leak, doesn't crash, and self-heals on error. A product chasing ambition without assurance collapses the harder the bigger it grows; one guarding assurance without daring ambition stays in place forever. A truly mature engineering rhythm has the two pace each other as they did this day: every big step forward in capability is matched by tamping down another layer of foundation. The sample made into a complete two-level campaign with refined visuals is the best footnote to this rhythm — it both shows how good it can get and, by running itself, proves whether all of it is truly stable.
