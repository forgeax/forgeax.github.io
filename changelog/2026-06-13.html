一个工程能放多个关卡、带启动器挑关;引擎阴影系统升到大场景阴影与点光源阴影;粒子与特效全套打磨。One project can hold multiple levels with a launcher; the engine's shadows step up to big-scene and point-light shadows; particles and effects get a full polish.

一个工程能放多个关卡 + 关卡启动器Multiple levels in one project + a level launcher

编辑器接入了"多场景游戏":同一个工程下可以放多个关卡文件,资产面板支持按名称组织子资产(不再一行行重复堆场景),还新增了一个启动器面板,让你在 Studio 里直接挑要进哪一关;编辑态和试玩态之间会成对同步,改完即看。配套的弹幕生存样例也做成了多关卡战役——白天牧场和夜晚墓地两关,作为"用一个工程做一整套关卡"的实战示范。做系列、做关卡分支,从此是顺理成章的事。The editor gained "multi-scene games": one project can hold multiple level files, the asset panel organizes sub-assets by name (no more stacking duplicate scene rows), and a new launcher panel lets you pick which level to enter right in Studio; edit and play stay paired in sync, so changes show immediately. The bullet-survival sample became a multi-level campaign too — a daytime pasture and a night graveyard — as a hands-on demonstration of "build a whole set of levels in one project." Making a series or branching levels is now a natural thing to do.

引擎阴影系统升档:大场景阴影 + 点光源阴影 + HDR/辉光A shadow-system upgrade: big-scene shadows + point-light shadows + HDR/bloom

引擎的阴影一次升了一档。新增"级联阴影"作为统一的阴影方案——这是大场景户外阴影的行业做法:近处阴影清晰、远处平滑过渡,大地图也不会糊。点光源现在也能投影了,一盏灯泡照出来的影子终于对得上。配合 HDR 与辉光的完整范例,以及一套把 GPU 资源用完即回收的生命周期管理(不再悄悄泄漏显存),画面质量和稳定性都更上一层。这些都是后续做有质感的真实场景的图形地基。The engine's shadows stepped up a level. A new "cascaded shadows" system became the unified approach — the industry technique for large outdoor scenes: crisp shadows up close, smooth transitions in the distance, so big maps don't go mushy. Point lights can now cast shadows too, so a bulb finally throws a shadow that matches it. Together with complete HDR and bloom examples, and a lifecycle system that reclaims GPU resources when done (no more quietly leaking video memory), both image quality and stability rose a notch. These are the graphics foundation for building believable real scenes from here.

粒子与特效全套打磨A full polish of particles and effects

围绕弹幕生存样例,把整条粒子与特效链路捋顺了:实例化的粒子系统(满屏特效也不卡)、加色与透明混合修对(再没有发黑的方块)、冲击波改成干净的薄环、技能各自的自定义着色器(爆炸 / 火焰 / 冰冻各有质感),再加上一层电影感的画面后处理,以及怪物与玩家角色的外观资产包。结果是这个样例从"能跑"变成"看着像个真游戏"——也顺带验证了整套特效工具的能力。Around the bullet-survival sample, the whole particle-and-effects chain was straightened out: an instanced particle system (no stutter even with effects filling the screen), additive and transparent blending fixed (no more blackened squares), shockwaves redone as clean thin rings, per-skill custom shaders (explosion / fire / ice each with their own feel), plus a layer of cinematic post-processing and appearance asset packs for the monsters and player character. The result is the sample going from "it runs" to "it looks like a real game" — and a working validation of the whole effects toolset along the way.
