引擎学会投影 + 新增叙事工作台The engine learns shadows + a new narrative workbench

物体开始投下阴影、各自带上自己的贴图;新增一个叙事工作台,角色工作台也能留住你生成的素材。Objects start casting shadows and carrying their own textures; a new narrative workbench arrives, and the character workbench keeps the assets you generate.

引擎学会投影:方向光阴影 + 逐物体贴图The engine learns shadows: directional-light shadows + per-object textures

引擎渲染迈过一道关键门槛——方向光阴影。物体现在会在方向光下投下阴影,也会接住别的物体投来的影子,场景的立体感和真实感一下子上来了:你能从阴影读出物体之间的高低与远近。配套还修好了逐物体的材质贴图绑定,同一个场景里不同物体可以各自贴各自的图,不再互相串。光照、阴影、贴图凑齐,引擎离能渲染一个"看上去对"的场景又近了一大步。Engine rendering crossed a key threshold — directional-light shadows. Objects now cast shadows under a directional light and receive shadows from others, which immediately lifts a scene's depth and realism: you can read height and distance between objects from their shadows. Alongside it, per-object material texture binding was fixed, so different objects in the same scene can each carry their own texture without bleeding into one another. With lighting, shadows and textures together, the engine is much closer to rendering a scene that simply "looks right."

新增叙事工作台A new narrative workbench

工具家族多了一位:叙事工作台。它以嵌入式工具窗的形态住进工作台,用来组织游戏的剧情与文本——把"做玩法"和"写故事"放进同一个平台里推进,而不是各用各的工具来回切。The tool family gained a new member: a narrative workbench. It lives in the workbench as an embedded tool window, used to organize a game's story and text — so "building gameplay" and "writing the story" advance inside one platform, instead of bouncing between separate tools.

角色工作台:生成的素材会留住,还能上传自己的Character workbench: keeps your assets, and takes your uploads

角色工作台更实用了:生成出来的立绘 / 素材现在会被保存下来,关掉再回来还在,不用每次重跑;分屏里两侧的内容也会保持同步。你还能上传自己的素材进来一起用,并按需路由到不同的生成服务——从"试着玩玩"变成"真能拿来做项目"。The character workbench got more practical: the portraits and assets it generates are now saved, so they're still there when you come back instead of having to re-run every time, and the two sides of a split view stay in sync. You can also upload your own assets to use alongside, with generation routed to different services as needed — moving it from "fun to try" toward "actually usable on a project."
