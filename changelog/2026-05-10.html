构建与运行时架构成型Build & runtime architecture takes shape

把"5 分钟跑起来"与一套可替换的分层架构设计落到纸面。Putting the "five-minute startup" and a replaceable, layered architecture down on paper.

五分钟跑起来Five minutes to running

确定了从零到本地跑通整套的路径:克隆、装依赖、一条命令把引擎与界面一起拉起来,几分钟内就能在浏览器里看到 Studio。这条"开箱即跑"的体验从第一天就被定为硬目标。We nailed down the path from zero to running locally — clone, install, and a single command brings up the engine and the interface together, so you're looking at the Studio in your browser within minutes. This "clone-and-run" experience was set as a hard goal from day one.

可替换的分层架构A replaceable, layered architecture

把整体拆成清晰、可替换的层——引擎、运行时、界面——并规划一个本地服务把它们编排起来。每一层都能独立演进、独立替换,为后面几周每天的快速迭代打好地基。We split the whole into clear, swappable layers — engine, runtime, interface — and planned a local service to orchestrate them. Each layer can evolve and be replaced on its own, laying the foundation for the fast day-by-day iteration of the weeks ahead.
