给游戏装上"验车线"A build-gate so the AI can't fake "done"

这天给 AI 造游戏装上了一道"验车线":构建跑不过,AI 就不许宣布"做完了",而且具体的报错会被喂回去让它照着改。同一天引擎大踏步推进——多材质模型、集群多光源、解除对象数量上限、更直觉的游戏入口写法;编辑器开始显示真实几何;团队进度也有了可视化图表。This day installed a build-gate on the AI's game-making: if the build doesn't pass, the AI isn't allowed to declare "done," and the specific errors are fed back for it to fix against. The same day the engine strode forward — multi-material models, clustered many-light rendering, an unlocked object cap, a more intuitive game-entry API; the editor began showing real geometry; and team progress gained visualization charts.

让"做完"变成一件可验证的事Making "done" a verifiable thing

把游戏开发交给 AI,有一个绕不开的根本问题:你怎么知道它说的"做完了"是真的做完了?人会下意识地相信"它说完成了就是完成了",但 AI 并不总是可靠——它可能改完代码就宣布大功告成,而你一打开却是黑屏。这天的主线,就是从机制上解决这个信任问题:不再让 AI"自己说了算",而是用一道客观的、机器执行的检验把关。这与同一天引擎那一大批底层增强放在一起看,恰好构成一组互补——验车线保证"它交付的东西真的能跑",引擎增强则保证"它能交付的东西更丰富、更大、更好看"。一边是质量的底线,一边是能力的上限,两头一起往前推,平台才既"靠得住"又"做得了"。Handing game development to an AI raises an unavoidable, fundamental question: how do you know its "done" is really done? People instinctively trust "if it says complete, it's complete," but an AI isn't always reliable — it might declare success right after editing code, only for you to open a black screen. The day's main line solves this trust problem mechanically: no longer letting the AI "be the judge of itself," but gatekeeping with an objective, machine-executed check. Seen alongside the same day's batch of engine enhancements, the two are complementary — the build-gate guarantees "what it delivers actually runs," while the engine enhancements guarantee "what it can deliver is richer, bigger and better-looking." One is the floor of quality, the other the ceiling of capability; pushing both forward is what makes the platform both "dependable" and "capable."

游戏"验车线":构建跑不过,AI 不许说做完A game build-gate: no "done" until it actually compiles

此前 AI 在这里"造游戏"常有个让人头疼的毛病:改完代码就宣布"完成",结果浏览器一打开是黑屏,甚至代码根本编译不过——你以为拿到了一个能玩的游戏,实际拿到的是一堆跑不起来的代码。这天给整个流程装上了一条"验车线":后端新增了一个构建预检环节,在 AI 宣布完成之前,先实打实地把游戏编译一遍;同时在 AI 的行为准则里写死了一条铁律——必须构建通过,才被允许宣布做完。光拦住还不够,关键是要让 AI 能自己修好:构建报错的具体内容会被实时转发到 AI 能看到的地方,把详细的错误原因喂回给它,而不是丢一句冷冰冰的"失败了"。于是一个完整的自我纠错闭环成形了:创建游戏 → AI 写代码 → 后端跑构建 → 失败就给出具体错误 → AI 看着错误再改 → 直到真的通过。这件事的深远意义在于:它把"做完"从一句 AI 的主观声明,变成了一个可被客观验证的事实——当它说"做好了",你能更放心地相信那是真的。The AI building games here used to have a headache-inducing flaw: it would declare "complete" after editing code, only for the browser to open a black screen, or for the code to not compile at all — you thought you got a playable game but actually got a pile of code that won't run. This day installed a build-gate across the whole flow: the backend added a build-preflight step that genuinely compiles the game before the AI declares completion; and a hard rule was baked into the AI's charter — it may only declare done once the build passes. Blocking alone isn't enough; the key is letting the AI fix it itself: the specific build errors are forwarded in real time to where the AI can see them, feeding back the detailed cause instead of a cold "it failed." So a complete self-correcting loop took shape: create game → AI writes code → backend runs build → failure yields specific errors → AI fixes against them → until it truly passes. The far-reaching significance: it turns "done" from an AI's subjective claim into an objectively verifiable fact — when it says "it's ready," you can trust that far more confidently.

多材质模型:复杂模型终于"各部位各材质"Multi-material models: each part finally gets its own material

引擎这天补上了一项处理复杂模型的关键能力:一个模型可以包含多个"子网格",每个子网格挂自己独立的材质槽。这听起来抽象,但用例非常具体——一个角色身上,盔甲是金属、衣服是布料、脸是皮肤,它们的质感、反光、颜色完全不同;一辆车,车身是喷漆、车窗是玻璃、轮胎是橡胶。此前模型往往只能整体套一种材质,这些不同部位的差异就被抹平,看起来要么全是塑料感、要么全是金属感,失真严重。现在引擎能把一个模型按部位拆成多个子网格,给每个部位分配各自的材质,复杂模型终于能正确地"各部位各材质"地渲染出来。配套还修了模型导入桥接的若干 bug,并让所有真实示例游戏跟着完成了数据格式的迁移。对创作者来说,这意味着从外部导入的那些精细模型,能以它们本来该有的样子被正确呈现,而不是被强行统一成一种材质。The engine added a key capability for handling complex models this day: a model can contain multiple "sub-meshes," each with its own material slot. It sounds abstract but the use case is very concrete — on a character, armor is metal, clothing is cloth, the face is skin, their texture, reflectivity and color entirely different; on a car, the body is painted, the windows glass, the tires rubber. Previously a model could often only wear one material as a whole, flattening these per-part differences so it looked either all-plastic or all-metal, badly distorted. Now the engine can split a model into multiple sub-meshes by part and assign each its own material, so complex models finally render correctly with "each part its own material." Several model-import bridging bugs were fixed alongside, and all the real sample games completed the data-format migration to match. For creators this means the detailed models imported from outside can be presented correctly as they were meant to look, rather than forced into one uniform material.

更多灯光、更大场景:把规模的天花板抬高More lights, bigger scenes: raising the ceiling on scale

引擎这天还从两个方向抬高了"场景能做多大"的天花板。一是光照:渲染走向了"集群光照"的路线——它的核心思路是把屏幕空间切分成许多小块,每块只计算真正影响到它的那几盏灯,从而让同一个场景里能容纳远比以前更多的光源而不至于拖垮性能。一个夜晚的城市、一个布满霓虹和路灯的街区,需要的正是大量灯光的同时存在,这条管线为这类场景打开了空间。二是容量:底层把存放对象数据的渲染缓冲改成了"按需翻倍扩容"——以前场景里的对象数量有一个大约一千的硬上限,超过就出问题;现在缓冲会随对象增多自动成倍扩张,这个上限被解除,大场景能塞下多得多的东西。这两项都是用户看不见、却直接决定"你的野心能做到多大"的底层功夫——它们本身不是某个具体玩法,而是让"更宏大的场景"这件事从不可能变为可能的前提。The engine also raised the ceiling on "how big a scene can be" from two directions this day. One is lighting: rendering moved to a "clustered lighting" approach — its core idea is to slice screen space into many small cells and, for each cell, compute only the few lights that actually affect it, so one scene can hold far more light sources than before without crushing performance. A nighttime city, a block full of neon and street lamps, needs exactly the simultaneous presence of many lights, and this pipeline opens room for such scenes. The other is capacity: the render buffers holding object data were changed to "grow on demand by doubling" — previously a scene's object count had a hard cap of roughly a thousand, beyond which it broke; now the buffer automatically expands by multiples as objects grow, the cap is lifted, and large scenes can hold much more. Both are under-the-hood work users don't see yet that directly decides "how big your ambition can go" — not a specific gameplay feature, but the precondition that turns "a grander scene" from impossible into possible.

更直觉的游戏入口写法A more intuitive way to write a game's entry point

引擎这天还把"创建一个游戏应用"的入口写法重新做了梳理,分成更直觉的三层:一层管摄像机与画面基本设置,一层管打包构建的选项,一层管资源的接入方式。此前这些不同性质的参数是混在一起的,写起来容易混淆——尤其对要靠它来写代码的 AI 而言,参数越杂、越没有清晰的归类,写错的概率就越高。把它按职责分成清楚的三层之后,每一层是干什么的一目了然,AI 写起游戏来更顺手、更不容易出错。这件事和当天的"验车线"其实是一体两面:验车线在出口处把关,堵住错误的产物;而把 API 设计得更直觉,则是在入口处降低出错的概率。一个让 AI 来写代码的平台,既要在事后能检验,也要在事前让它更难写错——两者配合,产出的质量才能稳步提升。新建游戏的默认模板也一并升级,开箱就自带 HDR 天空和一个能操控的角色,让每个新游戏都从一个更体面的起点开始。The engine also reorganized this day how "creating a game application" is written, splitting it into a more intuitive three layers: one for the camera and basic display settings, one for build/packaging options, one for how resources are wired in. Previously these differently-natured parameters were mixed together and easy to confuse — especially for the AI that relies on it to write code, the messier and less clearly-categorized the parameters, the higher the chance of error. Split by responsibility into three clear layers, what each layer does is obvious at a glance, so the AI writes games more smoothly and less error-prone. This and the day's "build-gate" are really two sides of one coin: the gate guards the exit, blocking faulty output; making the API more intuitive lowers the chance of error at the entrance. A platform that has the AI write code needs both to verify after the fact and to make it harder to err beforehand — together they steadily raise output quality. The default new-game template was upgraded too, shipping out of the box with an HDR sky and a controllable character, so every new game starts from a more presentable baseline.

编辑器显示真实几何 + 更稳的回放The editor shows real geometry + steadier replay

编辑器这天朝"所见即所得"又靠近了一步:现在它会预先加载模型文件,所以编辑视图里直接显示的是模型真实的几何形状,而不再是一个临时的占位方块。这看似细节,但影响很实在——你在编辑器里摆放、对齐、调整一个物体时,看到的就是它真正的样子,而不是凭空想象一个方块代表的东西最终会长成什么。底层资产管线也切到了引擎原生的场景包格式,编辑态同样带上了 HDR 天空,让编辑环境和最终运行环境进一步对齐。出错时,画面上的错误浮层会展开更详细的内层原因,而不只是一句笼统的报错,方便快速定位问题。此外还修了聊天回放里跨 agent 卡片的交错顺序——当你回看一段历史时,多个 AI 之间你来我往的发言能正确地穿插还原,和当时实时看到的顺序完全一致。这些改动共同服务于一件事:让你在编辑器里看到的、回放里看到的,都尽可能地接近真实。The editor moved a step closer to what-you-see-is-what-you-get this day: it now preloads model files, so the edit view directly shows a model's real geometry rather than a temporary placeholder box. It seems like a detail but the impact is real — when you place, align and adjust an object in the editor, you see what it actually looks like, instead of imagining what a box standing in for it will eventually become. The underlying asset pipeline also moved to the engine's native scene-pack format, and the edit view likewise carries an HDR sky, aligning the editing environment further with the final runtime. On error, the on-canvas overlay expands the more detailed underlying cause rather than a generic message, for quick diagnosis. Chat replay also fixed the interleaving order of cross-agent cards — when you review a stretch of history, the back-and-forth between multiple AIs is woven back together correctly, exactly matching the order you saw live. Together these serve one thing: making what you see in the editor, and in replay, as close to real as possible.

把团队的进度画成图Charting the team's progress

这天还给团队的工作进度加上了可视化图表。当越来越多的工作由 AI agent 并行推进时,"现在到底进行到哪了、还剩多少没做完"就成了一个需要被看见的问题。新增的吞吐量与燃尽曲线把这些信息画成了随时间累积的双线图,再配上一个项目指标面板,团队一眼就能看到整体的推进节奏:这段时间完成了多少、还有多少在路上、速度是在加快还是放缓。对一个高度依赖自动化的开发流程来说,这种"全局可见性"非常重要——人虽然把大量具体执行交给了 AI,但必须始终对整体态势保持掌控,知道事情正朝什么方向、以什么速度推进。把进度画成图,正是让人从繁杂的执行细节里抽身出来、仍能稳稳把握全局的一种手段。This day also added visualization charts for the team's work progress. As more and more work is pushed forward in parallel by AI agents, "where exactly are we now, and how much is left" becomes a question that needs to be seen. The added throughput and burn-down curves chart this information as dual lines accumulating over time, paired with a project-metrics panel, so the team sees the overall pace at a glance: how much was completed in this stretch, how much is still in flight, whether the speed is accelerating or slowing. For a development flow heavily reliant on automation, this "global visibility" matters greatly — though people hand much concrete execution to the AI, they must keep a grip on the overall situation, knowing in what direction and at what speed things are moving. Charting progress is precisely a means to step back from the tangle of execution details while still holding the big picture steady.

这一天意味着什么What this day means

这一天的两条主线,合起来回答了"如何让 AI 可靠地造出好游戏"这个核心命题的两个侧面。验车线回答的是"可靠":通过客观的构建检验和具体的错误反馈,把"做完"从一句空话变成一个能被验证、能被自我纠正的事实——这是信任的基石。引擎的一系列增强回答的是"好":多材质让模型更真实、集群光照与扩容让场景更宏大、更直觉的入口让 AI 更少出错——这是能力的拓展。一个由 AI 驱动的创作平台,最怕的就是"看起来很厉害,实际交付靠不住";而这一天恰恰是在同时夯实"靠得住"和"做得好"这两件最根本的事。当机器既能力更强、又被更严格地检验时,人才能真正放心地把"造一个游戏"这件事托付出去。The day's two main lines together answer two facets of the core proposition "how to make an AI reliably build good games." The build-gate answers "reliable": through objective build verification and specific error feedback, it turns "done" from empty words into a fact that can be verified and self-corrected — the cornerstone of trust. The engine's series of enhancements answer "good": multi-material makes models more real, clustered lighting and expanded capacity make scenes grander, a more intuitive entry makes the AI err less — the expansion of capability. What an AI-driven creation platform most fears is "looks impressive but delivers unreliably"; and this day firmed up exactly the two most fundamental things at once — "dependable" and "well-made." When the machine is both more capable and more strictly verified, people can truly, confidently entrust "building a game" to it.
