拖一个模型进来,自动变成场景Drag a model in, and it becomes a scene

这天的核心能力是:把一个 3D 模型文件拖进资产面板,它会被自动拆解成一个可编辑的场景。此前的痛点很具体——一个标准模型文件其实不是一坨孤立的几何,它内部往往带着完整的结构:节点的父子层级、一个个子网格、附带的灯光、甚至预设好的相机;但导进来后这些结构常常全丢了,你只剩一坨打不开、动不了的整体。现在的做法是:后端按需跑模型解析,把模型内部的节点树原样翻译成那份"以文档为准"的场景——模型里有什么层级、什么灯光、什么相机,场景里就还原出对应的对象,一一对应、可独立选中和编辑。配套在编辑界面加了导入下拉菜单,支持常见的模型、纹理与场景包格式;后端还新开了"上传二进制资源、按需解析模型、把节点树拆成场景文档"三个环节把这条链路接通,导入完成后资产面板自动刷新,不用手动重载。对创作者来说,这意味着你在外部捏好的一整个场景或一个带骨架的角色,拖进来就能直接接着摆、接着调,而不是从零重搭。The day's core capability: drag a 3D model file into the asset panel and it's automatically broken down into an editable scene. The prior pain was concrete — a standard model file isn't an isolated blob of geometry; inside it usually carries full structure: a node parent-child hierarchy, individual sub-meshes, attached lights, even preset cameras; but on import those structures were often all lost, leaving you a single opaque, immovable lump. The approach now: the backend runs model parsing on demand and translates the model's internal node tree faithfully into that "document-driven" scene — whatever hierarchy, lights and cameras the model holds, the scene restores the corresponding objects, one-to-one, individually selectable and editable. An import dropdown was added to the editing surface for common model, texture and scene-pack formats; the backend opened three steps — uploading binary assets, parsing the model on demand, and breaking the node tree into a scene document — to wire the path through, and the asset panel auto-refreshes after import with no manual reload. For creators this means a whole scene or a rigged character you sculpted externally can be dragged in and arranged and tuned right away, rather than rebuilt from scratch.

编辑器拆窗口不再乱跳:停靠系统大修Detaching panels no longer misbehaves: a docking overhaul

这天对编辑器的面板停靠做了一次彻底整修,一口气清掉了一堆积压已久的"抽风"行为。此前的痛点是:多窗口操作处处是坑——把检视器之类的面板拖出成一个独立的操作系统窗口、再把那个窗口关掉、或者在分离出去的面板里按撤销/重做,都可能各种乱跳、错位甚至卡死。根因在于停靠的内部结构层层嵌套、状态难以追踪。这天把它改成了"扁平结构":预览和编辑都被提成顶层的独立面板,层级一拉平,行为就好预测了。在这套新结构上,一连串具体毛病被逐个修好:把面板拖到空白处会干脆利落地弹成独立窗口,而不是含糊地浮在应用内;独立窗口被关掉时,里面的面板会自己乖乖回到主界面;撤销/重做在分离的面板里也终于正常工作。视觉上还把那个有点刺眼的蓝色标签高亮换成了中性的深色,面板名称也统一规整。多窗口工作流从"能用但提心吊胆"变成了"真的稳"。This day gave the editor's panel docking a thorough overhaul, clearing in one go a long-accumulated pile of "glitchy" behavior. The prior pain: multi-window operations were full of traps — dragging a panel like the inspector out into its own OS window, then closing that window, or undoing/redoing in a detached panel, could all glitch, misalign or even freeze. The root cause was the docking's deeply-nested internal structure with hard-to-track state. This day changed it to a "flat structure": preview and edit were promoted to top-level independent panels, and flattening the hierarchy made behavior predictable. On this new structure, a string of specific bugs were fixed one by one: dragging a panel to empty space cleanly pops it into its own window instead of vaguely floating within the app; when an independent window is closed, the panel inside dutifully returns to the main interface; and undo/redo finally works in detached panels. Visually, the somewhat harsh blue tab highlight was swapped for a neutral dark tone, and panel names were tidied and unified. The multi-window workflow went from "usable but nerve-wracking" to "genuinely stable."

天空盒回归:HDR 环境贴图导入修好The skybox returns: HDR environment-map import fixed

前一阵子,负责加载 HDR 环境贴图的那条链路断过一段时间,直接后果是场景里的天空一度变成了黑的——天光、环境氛围全没了。这天把整套环境贴图的导入与加载补了回来,HDR 天空在编辑和试玩里都能重新正常出图。这里值得解释一下它为什么重要:一张 HDR 环境贴图不只是"画一张好看的天空背景"那么简单,它同时是整个场景的光照来源——天空什么颜色、哪里亮哪里暗,会自然地照在场景里所有物体上,决定整个画面的明暗冷暖。所以天空一黑,丢的不只是背景,而是整个场景的"光的氛围"。把这条链路修回来,等于把场景的"灵魂照明"重新点亮。这也提醒了一件事:越是这种牵一发动全身的基础链路,越要有回归防护,免得它再次悄悄断掉而没人立刻发现。A while back, the chain responsible for loading HDR environment maps had broken for a stretch, with the direct consequence that the sky in scenes went black for a time — sky light and ambient atmosphere all gone. This day restored the whole environment-map import and loading path, so the HDR sky renders correctly again in both edit and play. It's worth explaining why this matters: an HDR environment map isn't just "drawing a pretty sky backdrop"; it's simultaneously the lighting source for the entire scene — what color the sky is, where it's bright and where dim, naturally falls on every object in the scene and decides the whole image's light and shade, warm and cool. So when the sky goes black, what's lost isn't just the backdrop but the scene's "atmosphere of light." Restoring this chain relights the scene's "soul lighting." It's also a reminder: the more a foundational chain affects everything downstream, the more it needs regression protection, lest it quietly break again with no one noticing right away.

为"同一模型摆很多个"打底Laying groundwork for "placing many copies of one model"

引擎这天还在底层做了一件为性能铺路的事:让多个着色器接上专门的"逐实例变换"通道。这是什么意思?很多游戏场景里会出现大量同一个模型的重复——一片树林里的几百棵树、一面墙上的成排砖块、一群外形相同的敌人。最朴素的做法是把这个模型逐个重新画一遍,但那样开销会随数量线性暴涨,场景一大就卡。更高效的做法叫"实例化":同一个模型只交给显卡一次,再附上一张"每个副本各自的位置、朝向、缩放"的清单,让显卡一次性把成百上千个副本各就各位地画出来。这天把场景中多个着色器接上了这条逐实例的数据通道,并让阴影绘制也与之对齐、加上了回归防护。这属于用户看不见、但直接决定"场景能堆多大、跑多流畅"的底层功夫——它本身不是一个功能,而是让后面很多"大场景"功能成为可能的前提。The engine also did a performance-paving thing underneath this day: wiring several shaders to a dedicated "per-instance transform" channel. What does this mean? Many game scenes feature heavy repetition of the same model — hundreds of trees in a forest, rows of bricks on a wall, a crowd of identically-shaped enemies. The naivest approach redraws the model one by one, but that cost balloons linearly with count and a large scene stutters. The more efficient approach is "instancing": the same model is handed to the GPU just once, attached with a list of "each copy's own position, orientation and scale," letting the GPU draw hundreds or thousands of copies into their places in one pass. This day wired several shaders in the scene to this per-instance data channel, aligned shadow drawing with it, and added regression protection. This is the kind of under-the-hood work users don't see but that directly decides "how big a scene can be stacked and how smoothly it runs" — not a feature in itself but the precondition that makes many later "large-scene" features possible.

如果说前几天是把编辑器"盖起来",这一天就是给它"通水电、修地基"。导入能力让编辑器不再是一座只能用自家材料的孤岛,而是一个能接纳外部资产、继续在其上创作的开放工作台——这一步极大地拓宽了创作者能用的素材范围。停靠系统大修则是另一种价值:它没有增加任何新功能,纯粹是把已有的东西从"能用但常出岔子"打磨到"用着踏实",而这种踏实恰恰是专业工具能被长期信赖的根本。再加上天空照明的回归和实例化渲染的铺垫,这一天把编辑器的"进料、稳定、视觉、性能"四个侧面同时往前推了一格。它提醒我们:一个工具走向成熟,既要不断长出新能力,也要不断回头把旧能力夯到真正可靠——两者缺一不可。If the preceding days were about "building the editor up," this day was about "running its water and power and firming its foundation." The import capability makes the editor no longer an island that can only use its own materials but an open workbench that can take in external assets and keep creating on top of them — a step that vastly broadens the range of materials creators can use. With the return of sky lighting and the groundwork for instanced rendering, this day pushed the editor's "intake, stability, visuals, performance" four facets forward a notch at once. It reminds us: a tool matures by both continually growing new capabilities and continually going back to firm old ones into true reliability — neither dispensable.
