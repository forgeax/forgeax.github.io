拖一个模型进来,自动变成场景Drag a model in, and it becomes a scene

编辑器学会了一件很实用的事:把一个 3D 模型文件拖进资产面板,它会被自动拆解成一个可编辑的场景——模型里的节点层级、子网格、灯光、相机都被原样还原成场景里的对象,而不再是"一坨打不开的模型"。编辑界面新增了导入下拉菜单,支持常见的模型、纹理与场景包格式;导入完成后资产面板会自动刷新,不用手动重载。把外面做好的资产搬进来继续编排,从此是顺手一拖的事。The editor learned something very practical: drag a 3D model file into the asset panel and it's automatically broken down into an editable scene — the model's node hierarchy, sub-meshes, lights and cameras are restored as objects in the scene, instead of staying "one opaque blob of a model." The editing surface gained an import dropdown supporting common model, texture and scene-pack formats; after import the asset panel auto-refreshes, with no manual reload. Bringing assets made elsewhere in to keep arranging them is now a single drag.

编辑器拆窗口不再乱跳:停靠系统大修Detaching editor panels no longer misbehaves: a docking overhaul

编辑器的面板停靠做了一次彻底整修。以前把检视器之类的面板拖到独立窗口、再关掉、或者撤销重做,常会出各种乱跳和卡死。现在停靠改成扁平结构:预览和编辑都是顶层独立面板,你可以把任意面板拖出成独立的操作系统窗口,关掉它会自动回坞;撤销/重做在分离出去的面板里也正常工作了。视觉上把刺眼的蓝色高亮换成了中性色,面板命名也统一。多窗口工作流终于稳了。The editor's panel docking got a thorough overhaul. Previously, dragging a panel like the inspector into its own window, then closing it, or undoing/redoing, often led to glitches and freezes. Docking is now a flat structure: preview and edit are top-level independent panels, you can drag any panel out into its own OS window, and closing it re-docks automatically; undo/redo works in detached panels too. Visually, the harsh blue highlight was swapped for a neutral color and panel names were unified. The multi-window workflow is finally stable.

天空盒回归:HDR 环境贴图导入修好The skybox returns: HDR environment import fixed

前阵子 HDR 环境贴图的加载链断过一段时间,场景里的天空一度是黑的。这天把整套环境贴图导入与加载补了回来,HDR 天空在编辑和试玩里都能正常出图了——场景重新有了天光氛围。底层还为逐实例变换接了专门的着色器通道,为同一模型大量重复摆放时各有各的位置朝向打好基础。For a while the HDR environment-map loading chain had broken, and the sky in scenes went black. This day restored the whole environment-map import and loading path, so the HDR sky renders correctly again in both edit and play — scenes have their sky atmosphere back. Underneath, a dedicated shader channel was wired for per-instance transforms, laying groundwork for placing many copies of the same model each with its own position and orientation.
