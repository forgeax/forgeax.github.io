This day, those parts were assembled into the same real game for the first time — a dark-fantasy action-RPG sample with a moving witch hero, switchable cameras, naturally-blended combat and nighttime atmosphere. It's a meaningful milestone: it proves those scattered capabilities aren't just individually demonstrable but can genuinely combine to support a flesh-and-blood game. The day's other lines all orbited "making creation smoother": turning model upload into an immediately-usable end-to-end flow, letting you roll back to any step of the conversation at any time, and paving the way for the engine to ingest professional modeling formats. In one line: moving from "the parts are ready" to "they assemble into a whole machine," and continuing to polish the assembly to be smoother.

新增暗黑风格 ARPG 样例:HellforgeA new dark-fantasy ARPG sample: Hellforge

这天新增了一个完整的暗黑风格动作角色扮演(ARPG)样例游戏。它的女巫主角是一个带骨骼的角色模型,自带五段动画——待机、移动、攻击等,这正是前几天打通的骨骼动画第一次被用在一个真实的可玩角色身上,女巫不再是站着不动的模型,而是会走、会打的活角色。视角上提供了两种并用一个按键切换:一种是贴在角色身后的越肩跟随视角,一种是经典 ARPG 那样略微俯瞰的 2.5D 视角;战斗动作由一套状态机驱动,让"待机→移动→攻击"之间的切换衔接自然而不生硬。场景配了夜视氛围光——昏暗的月光加上几支营地火炬,把暗黑风格的气氛烘托出来。这个样例的价值在于,它是平台第一次把骨骼动画、相机系统、战斗逻辑、氛围光照这些此前分头打磨的能力,全部串进同一个有完整玩法的游戏里。它既是一个能直接上手玩的成品,也是任何想做同类动作角色扮演游戏的人可以直接站上去的现成起点。This day a complete dark-fantasy action-RPG sample game was added. Its witch hero is a rigged character model with five animations — idle, move, attack and more — which is the first time the recently-completed skeletal animation was used on a real playable character; the witch is no longer a standing-still model but a living character who walks and fights. It offers two cameras switchable with a single key: an over-shoulder follow camera behind the character, and a slightly top-down 2.5D view like classic ARPGs; combat actions are driven by a state machine so the transitions between "idle → move → attack" blend naturally rather than stiffly. The scene carries night-vision atmosphere — dim moonlight plus a few camp torches — evoking the dark-fantasy mood. The sample's value is that it's the first time the platform threaded the separately-polished capabilities of skeletal animation, the camera system, combat logic and atmosphere lighting all into one game with complete gameplay. It's both a directly playable finished product and a ready starting point anyone wanting to make a similar action-RPG can stand right on.

编辑器更独立 + 上传模型立刻能用A more independent editor + upload a model and use it now

这天最直接惠及创作者的一件事,是把"拿来一个模型 → 立刻用进游戏"做成了端到端的一条线。试玩端补齐了完整的模型导入,而且覆盖一个模型内部的全部子内容——网格(形状)、材质(质感)、纹理(表面图案)、动画(动作)、骨骼(可驱动的骨架)等等,一样都不漏。这意味着你上传一个模型,马上就能在试玩里看到它、用上它,带骨骼的角色也能在编辑视图里正确预览;不必再为"导进来却显示不全、或者动画 / 骨骼丢了"而发愁。为了让带骨骼的模型能被正确读入,底层还专门让网格的校验"懂"了骨骼数据——带蒙皮的顶点比普通顶点多带骨骼权重信息,数据排布不一样,校验必须能分辨这两种,否则骨骼模型一进来就会被判为格式错误。与此同时,编辑器把前端外壳的核心框架整个吸收了进来、删掉了自建的旧停靠层,从此能更彻底地独立装载、不必和主聊天界面绑死——这为后面把编辑器嵌进别处、或单独打包留好了空间。The thing most directly benefiting creators this day was making "bring in a model → use it in the game right away" one end-to-end path. The play side gained complete model import, covering all of a model's internal sub-contents — mesh (shape), material (texture quality), texture (surface pattern), animation (motion), skeleton (the drivable bones) and more, missing none. This means you upload a model and can immediately see and use it in play, and rigged characters preview correctly in the edit view too; no more fretting over "it imported but doesn't display fully, or the animation/skeleton was lost." To read rigged models correctly, the underlying mesh validation was made to "understand" skeleton data — skinned vertices carry bone-weight information beyond ordinary vertices and are laid out differently, and validation must distinguish the two, or a skinned model would be flagged as malformed on import. Meanwhile, the editor absorbed the core of the front-end shell's framework and removed its old self-built docking layer, so it can now mount more fully independently and isn't bound to the main chat interface — leaving room to later embed the editor elsewhere or package it on its own.

可回退到任意一条历史消息 + 逐文件 diffRoll back to any past message + per-file diffs

这天后端加了一项很安心的能力:消息级的回退点。和 AI 来回试错时,常常会遇到"前几轮改对了,但最近这几轮越改越歪"的情况——这天起,每一步改动都会被打上快照,你可以"软回退"到此前任意一条消息时的状态,把最近几轮不满意的改动一键退掉、重新来过。关键是它是"软"的、可逆的:回退不会把后面的东西硬删掉,你随时还能再回到更晚的状态,等于给整个创作过程装了一条可以来回拖动的时间轴。界面上把每个回退点、被退掉而置灰的部分、以及当前的编辑状态都清楚地呈现出来,还能看到每一步到底改动了哪些文件、各自改了多少行的逐文件明细。这件事的意义在于,它把"和 AI 协作"从一件需要小心翼翼、生怕改坏的事,变成了一件可以大胆试的事——因为无论怎么试,你都随时退得回来。安全网越牢,人就越敢探索。This day the backend added a reassuring capability: message-level rollback points. In trial-and-error with the AI, you often hit "the earlier rounds were right, but the recent rounds drifted worse and worse" — from this day, every step of changes gets snapshotted, and you can "soft-revert" to the state at any earlier message, rolling back the last few unsatisfactory rounds in one click and starting over. The key is that it's "soft" and reversible: a rollback doesn't hard-delete what came after, and you can always return to a later state again, effectively fitting the whole creative process with a timeline you can drag back and forth. The interface clearly surfaces each rollback point, the dimmed parts that were rolled back, and the current edit state, and shows a per-file breakdown of exactly which files each step changed and by how many lines. The significance is that it turns "collaborating with the AI" from something requiring caution lest you break things into something you can boldly try — because however you try, you can always come back. The sturdier the safety net, the bolder people dare to explore.

引擎:为专业模型格式铺路 + 录像回放调试Engine: paving the way for professional model formats + record-and-replay debugging

引擎这天为吃下专业建模工具直出的模型格式做了设计与准备。专业的三维建模软件通常导出一种能完整携带动画、骨骼、甚至物理参数的格式,而平台目前主要支持的是另一种网页通用格式——很多专业资产要先做一次格式转换才能用进来,转换过程难免有损耗、丢细节。这天的设计就是要让引擎能直接读入专业格式,省掉中间转换的折腾,让从专业工具流出的资产原汁原味地进入游戏。另一项重磅是落了一套"录像 → 回放对比"的调试基础设施:它能把渲染过程录下来,在不同设备上逐像素回放比对,专门用来抓"同一段代码在这台机器上画得不一样"那类极难复现的回归问题。此外还接入了环境光遮蔽(让墙角、缝隙这些光照不到的地方自然地变暗,画面立体感更强),并修掉了一个关键 bug——此前开启抗锯齿时,会悄无声息地让自定义材质的着色器失效;这种"沉默地坏掉"最危险,因为你根本不知道哪里出了问题,这天把它合上了。The engine designed and prepared to ingest the model formats that professional modeling tools output directly this day. Professional 3D modeling software usually exports a format that fully carries animation, skeleton and even physics parameters, while the platform currently mainly supports another web-common format — many professional assets must undergo a format conversion before use, and conversion inevitably loses detail. This day's design is for the engine to read professional formats directly, skipping the intermediate-conversion hassle so assets flowing from professional tools enter the game in their original fidelity. Another heavyweight was landing a "record → replay and compare" debugging infrastructure: it can record the rendering process and replay it pixel-by-pixel across different devices for comparison, purpose-built to catch the very-hard-to-reproduce regressions where "the same code draws differently on this machine." It also wired in ambient occlusion (naturally darkening corners and crevices that light can't reach, for stronger depth), and fixed a critical bug — previously, turning on anti-aliasing silently disabled custom material shaders; such "silent breakage" is the most dangerous because you have no idea where it went wrong, and this day closed it.

这一天最值得记住的,是那个暗黑风格 ARPG 样例所标志的转折:平台不再只是"能做出会动的角色""能导入模型""能投影打光"这些孤立的能力点,而是第一次把它们拼成了一个有主角、有视角、有战斗、有氛围的完整游戏。能力点的价值,最终要靠它们能不能合奏成一首完整的曲子来兑现——这一天给出了一个响亮的肯定回答。而围绕它的另外几件事,则都在为"让更多人更顺地拼出自己的游戏"使劲:上传模型立刻能用,降低了用素材的门槛;可回退到任意消息,降低了试错的心理负担;为专业格式铺路,拓宽了能接入的资产来源;录像回放,则让画面问题更容易被抓住和修复。从攒零件到拼整机,再到把拼装的每一步都打磨顺手——这一天,平台朝"让任何人都能拼出一个真正的游戏"这个目标,迈出了扎实的一大步。The most memorable thing this day is the turning point the dark-fantasy ARPG sample marks: the platform is no longer just isolated capability points like "can make a moving character," "can import models," "can cast lighting and shadows," but assembled them, for the first time, into a complete game with a hero, cameras, combat and atmosphere. The value of capability points is ultimately redeemed by whether they can play together as a complete tune — and this day gave a resounding yes. The other things around it all pushed toward "letting more people assemble their own games more smoothly": instantly-usable model upload lowered the bar to using assets; rollback to any message lowered the psychological burden of trial and error; paving the way for professional formats broadened the sources of assets that can come in; and record-and-replay made visual problems easier to catch and fix. From accumulating parts to assembling a whole machine, to polishing each step of the assembly to be smooth — this day took a solid step toward the goal of "letting anyone assemble a real game."
