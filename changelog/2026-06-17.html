把"常用的轮子"沉到引擎里Sinking "the wheels everyone reinvents" into the engine

这一天有一条清晰的主线:把那些"每做一个游戏都要重复造一遍"的常用轮子,沉淀成引擎和模板里现成的能力。角色撞墙会滑的标准移动、待机跑跳的状态机,是每个动作游戏都绕不开的基础;一个样例关卡踩过的坑,被写成复盘、再反哺进默认模板,让后来者不必重蹈覆辙;编辑器的独立窗口被打磨到日常可用,成了一个稳定的工具底座。除此之外,这天还往外伸了两只手:让 Forge 能从你平时用的聊天软件里直接对话,以及让 AI 能"看见"运行中的游戏画面与状态。前者降低门槛、沉淀通用能力,后者拓展边界、让 AI 离"真正理解一个游戏"更近一步。把重复的东西做成通用的、把通用的东西做扎实,正是一个平台积累复利的方式。This day had a clear through-line: distilling the "wheels everyone reinvents for every game" into ready-made capabilities in the engine and templates. Standard slide-along-walls movement and an idle-run-jump state machine are unavoidable basics for every action game; a sample level's pitfalls were written into a retrospective and fed back into the default template so others needn't repeat them; and the standalone editor window was polished to daily-usable, becoming a stable tool base. Beyond that, the day reached two hands outward: letting Forge be talked to from the chat apps you already use, and letting the AI "see" the running game's picture and state. The former lowers the bar and distills general capabilities; the latter extends the boundary, bringing the AI a step closer to "truly understanding a game." Turning the repeated into the general, and making the general solid, is exactly how a platform compounds.

"撞墙会滑、不卡死"的标准角色移动 + 状态机Standard "slide, don't stick" movement + a state machine

引擎这天落地了一个做任何"能跑能跳"游戏都离不开的基础原语:运动学角色控制器,也就是"移动并滑动",2D 和 3D 都支持。它解决的是一个看似简单、却极其影响手感的问题——当角色斜着撞向一堵墙时该怎么办?最糟的处理是直接卡死(角色像被钉在墙上),或者干脆穿墙而过(穿模);而正确的、玩家习以为常的做法,是让角色沿着墙面顺滑地滑行过去。这种"贴着墙滑"的移动看似不起眼,却是几乎所有第一人称、第三人称游戏的手感底线,自己从零实现却相当繁琐。把它做成引擎内置的标准原语,等于让每一个新游戏都直接拥有及格的移动手感。配套还做了一个类型安全的状态机:把角色的"待机 / 跑动 / 跳跃 / 攻击"这类状态规范地表达出来,并且让实体的生命周期能和状态绑定——进入某个状态时自动生成相关的东西、离开时自动清理。这两件加在一起,意味着下一个游戏的角色控制与行为逻辑,不必每次都从一张白纸开始手搓,而是站在一套经过验证的基础之上。The engine landed a building block no "run and jump" game can do without this day: a kinematic character controller — "move and slide" — for both 2D and 3D. It solves a seemingly simple yet hugely feel-affecting problem: what happens when a character hits a wall at an angle? The worst handling jams it dead (the character pinned to the wall) or lets it pass through (clipping); the correct, player-expected handling slides the character smoothly along the surface. This "slide along the wall" movement seems minor but is the floor of feel for nearly all first- and third-person games, and quite tedious to implement from scratch. Making it a built-in standard primitive gives every new game passable movement feel for free. Alongside came a type-safe state machine: cleanly expressing a character's states like idle / run / jump / attack, and binding entity lifecycles to states — auto-spawning related things on entering a state, auto-cleaning on leaving. Together, the next game's character control and behavior logic needn't be hand-rolled from a blank page each time but stand on a validated foundation.

编辑器独立窗口成熟可用The standalone editor window is ready for daily use

前一天重写的编辑器独立窗口,这天被推到了能日常使用的成熟度。具体打磨了好几处:停靠面板改用弹性布局,能自动铺满整个视口而不留空白;移除了一个一直残留在那里的"幽灵空画布"(之前界面上总有一块莫名其妙的空白渲染区);斩断了模块之间的循环依赖,让架构更清爽、更不容易出连锁问题;独立窗口现在自己注入编辑与预览两套渲染,不再依赖主界面。控制台的健康度也修到了只剩正常的资源缺失提示,不再是满屏红字让人心里没底。此外还补齐了双语说明文档、把启动方式写清楚、配上结构图,确保一个新人照着文档就能在自己机器上把它单独跑起来。经过这一天,编辑器作为一个可以脱离主界面、独立打开使用的工具,真正站稳了——这为将来把它嵌进别处或单独分发,打下了可靠的基础。The standalone editor window rewritten the day before was pushed to daily-use maturity this day. Several things were polished: docked panels switched to a flexible layout that fills the whole viewport with no blank gaps; a "ghost empty canvas" that had lingered there was removed (the interface used to have an inexplicable blank render area); the circular dependency between modules was broken, making the architecture cleaner and less prone to cascading problems; and the standalone window now injects its own edit and preview rendering, no longer depending on the main interface. Console health was fixed down to just the normal missing-resource notices, no more screens of red leaving you uneasy. Bilingual docs were completed, the startup method written out clearly with a structure diagram, ensuring a newcomer can stand it up on their own machine by following along. After this day, the editor truly holds its own as a tool you can open and use detached from the main interface — laying a reliable foundation to later embed it elsewhere or distribute it separately.

样例关卡修通,把教训反哺进模板Sample levels fixed, lessons fed back into the template

一个样例关卡此前连环踩了三个坑,这天被一次扫清,而处理方式比"修好"本身更值得说。三个坑分别是:场景里对象的内部编号不连续,导致走位和视角操作失灵——通过把编号重排成连续序列修好;阴影用的还是旧的配置方案——迁移到新的阴影方案并锁定一套稳定的渲染配置;以及由此暴露出的一些过时的使用约定。关键在于第三步:团队没有止步于"把这一关修好",而是把整个翻车与排查的过程写成了一份复盘文档,并据此修订了相关的使用约定,更进一步——把这些教训反哺进了新建游戏的默认模板里的"易踩坑清单"。这意味着同样的坑,后来的每一个游戏都不会再踩。这正是一个成熟工程团队对待 bug 的态度:一个 bug 修好只是最低要求,把它背后的教训制度化、让它再也不会以同样的形式出现,才是真正的收尾。同一天还把样例主角的蒙皮动画接通,让它从一个静止的模型变成真正会动的角色。A sample level had hit three pitfalls in a row, all cleared this day — and the way they were handled is more notable than the fix itself. The three were: the scene objects' internal IDs weren't contiguous, breaking movement and camera controls — fixed by renumbering them into a consecutive sequence; shadows still used an old configuration scheme — migrated to the new shadow approach with a stable render config locked in; and some outdated usage conventions thereby exposed. The key is the third step: the team didn't stop at "fix this level" but wrote the whole derailment and diagnosis into a retrospective, revised the relevant usage conventions accordingly, and went further — feeding these lessons back into the "common pitfalls" list in the default new-game template. This means every later game won't fall into the same trap again. This is exactly a mature engineering team's attitude toward bugs: fixing a bug is the minimum; institutionalizing the lesson behind it so it never recurs in the same form is the real closure. The same day, the sample hero's skinned animation was wired up, turning it from a static model into a genuinely moving character.

从常用聊天软件里直接对话 ForgeTalk to Forge directly from common chat apps

这天还打开了一个新的入口:让你能从平时常用的即时通讯软件里,直接和 Forge 对话。界面上新增了一个"连接"设置页,用来把这些外部聊天渠道接进来,你扫个码或授权一下,就能在自己熟悉的聊天软件里直接给 Forge 发消息、让它帮你推进游戏开发。这件事的意义在于,它把"和 AI 协作"从"必须坐在电脑前打开 Studio 界面"中解放了出来——你可以在通勤路上、在手机上,随手丢给它一个想法或一条指令。对一个以对话为核心交互的平台来说,"能在任何地方、用任何顺手的渠道找到它",本身就是一种重要的可达性。降低"开始一次对话"的门槛,意味着更多灵感能在产生的那一刻就被记录下来、被推进,而不是等回到电脑前早已忘记。This day also opened a new entry point: letting you talk to Forge directly from the instant-messaging apps you commonly use. A "Connections" settings page was added to wire in these external chat channels — scan a code or authorize, and you can message Forge right inside your familiar chat app and have it push your game development forward. The significance is that it frees "collaborating with the AI" from "must sit at the computer and open the Studio interface" — you can toss it an idea or a command on the commute, on your phone. For a platform whose core interaction is conversation, "being reachable anywhere, through any convenient channel" is itself important accessibility. Lowering the bar to "starting a conversation" means more inspiration can be captured and advanced the moment it arises, rather than forgotten by the time you're back at the computer.

让 AI 能"看见"运行中的游戏Letting the AI "see" the running game

引擎这天还埋下了一个意义深远的基础:一套让 AI 能"感知"运行中游戏的协议。说得具体些,就是给 AI 配了两类新工具——一类能查询游戏世界当前的状态(场景里有哪些对象、它们各自在什么位置、什么状态),一类能抓取当前的画面截图。这意味着 AI 不再只能"盲写代码"——它写完之后,可以真正"看一眼"游戏现在跑成什么样、对象摆得对不对、画面有没有问题,然后据此判断和调整。这是 AI 从"只会写"走向"会写、会看、会自我纠正"的关键一步。配套还提供了一种无界面的方式来驱动这套流程,方便把它接入自动化。对一个目标是"让 AI 自主开发游戏"的平台来说,"让 AI 拥有眼睛"几乎是必经之路——只有当它能感知到自己改动的实际效果,它的自主纠错才能真正闭环,而不是凭空猜测。这天落的是地基切片,但它指向的方向非常明确。The engine also planted a far-reaching foundation this day: a protocol letting the AI "perceive" the running game. Concretely, it gave the AI two new kinds of tools — one to query the game world's current state (what objects are in the scene, where each is, what state it's in), and one to capture a screenshot of the current picture. This means the AI is no longer limited to "writing code blind" — after writing, it can genuinely "take a look" at how the game runs now, whether objects are placed right, whether the picture has problems, and judge and adjust accordingly. This is a key step for the AI moving from "can only write" to "writes, sees, and self-corrects." A headless way to drive this flow was provided too, for hooking into automation. For a platform aiming to "have the AI develop games autonomously," "giving the AI eyes" is almost an unavoidable path — only when it can perceive the actual effect of its own changes can its autonomous self-correction truly close the loop, rather than guessing blind. What landed this day is a foundation slice, but the direction it points is very clear.

这一天意味着什么What this day means

这一天的几件事,合起来体现了一个平台积累复利的两种方式:沉淀,与延展。沉淀,是把那些反复出现的东西收进底层,变成所有人都能直接用的现成能力——标准的角色移动、可复用的状态机、被反哺进模板的避坑经验、日常可用的独立编辑器,都是在做这件事。每沉淀一样,后来的每一个游戏就少造一次轮子,平台的"做游戏"就快一分、稳一分。延展,是往新的方向伸出触角——让 Forge 能从聊天软件里被随手找到,让 AI 能看见自己改动的实际效果。一个把"撞墙会滑"这种基础原语都认真做扎实、又同时在为"AI 拥有眼睛"埋下基础的平台,既脚踏实地,又眼望远方。把重复的做通用、把通用的做可靠、再在可靠之上探索新的可能——这一天,正是这条复利路径上扎实的一步。The day's items together embody two ways a platform compounds: distillation and extension. Distillation sinks the recurring into the foundation, into ready-made capabilities everyone can use directly — standard character movement, a reusable state machine, pitfall-avoiding lessons fed into the template, a daily-usable standalone editor all do this. Each thing distilled means one fewer wheel reinvented for every later game, and the platform's "making games" gets a notch faster and steadier. Extension reaches feelers in new directions — making Forge reachable on a whim from chat apps, letting the AI see the actual effect of its own changes. A platform that earnestly makes even a basic primitive like "slide along walls" solid while laying the foundation for "the AI having eyes" is both grounded and far-sighted. Making the repeated general, the general reliable, and exploring new possibilities atop the reliable — this day was a solid step on that compounding path.
