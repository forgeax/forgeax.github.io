把内容浏览器和拖拽到场景对标 UE5 + 编辑器自带质检门A UE5-grade plan for the content browser & drag-to-scene, plus an editor QA gate

这是一个"打地基"的日子,没有炫目的新玩法,做的是两件让后面走得更稳的事:一是把"资产怎么浏览、怎么拖进场景"按成熟引擎(UE5)的标准写成白纸黑字的设计,二是给独立编辑器装上一道属于它自己的自动质检门,让它往后长新功能时不会悄悄把旧的弄坏。A foundations day — no flashy new gameplay, but two things that make everything after it steadier: first, writing down a UE5-grade design for how assets get browsed and dragged into a scene; second, putting the standalone editor under its own automated quality gate, so it can grow new features without quietly breaking the old ones.

为什么"先写设计、再动手"Why "design first, then build"

越是要做得像样、越是要对标成熟工具的功能,越值得先把它想清楚、写下来,再开工。内容浏览器和"把模型拖进场景"听起来简单,但真正决定好不好用的,是无数细节:资产怎么分类、缩略图怎么生成、拖进去的瞬间放在哪、朝向和缩放怎么定、和已有物体怎么对齐。这些如果边做边拍脑袋,最后往往各处不一致、改起来牵一发动全身。这一天先把这套交互按"对标 UE5"的标准整理成设计文档:以业界公认顺手的范式为参照,先定清楚"它应该是什么样",再让 AI 照着实现。对一个主要由 AI 自主推进的项目来说,一份清晰的设计文档尤其关键——它既是给 AI 的施工图,也是事后判断"做对没做对"的标尺。The more polished something needs to be — and the more it aims to match a mature tool's feature — the more it's worth thinking through and writing down before building. A content browser and "drag a model into the scene" sound simple, but what really decides whether they feel good is a thousand details: how assets are categorized, how thumbnails are generated, where a dropped item lands, how its orientation and scale are decided, how it snaps to existing objects. Improvised on the fly, these tend to end up inconsistent and entangled to change. This day first organized the interaction into a design doc benchmarked against UE5: using the industry's proven, comfortable patterns as the reference, pinning down "what it should be" before letting the AI implement it. For a project driven largely by AI, a clear design doc matters all the more — it is both the construction drawing for the AI and the yardstick for judging afterward whether it was done right.

对标 UE5 的内容浏览器与"拖进场景"A content browser & drag-to-scene, benchmarked against UE5

这份设计瞄准的是创作里最高频的两个动作:找到一个资产、把它放进世界里。"内容浏览器"对标的是成熟引擎里那个分门别类、带缩略图、能搜索筛选的资产库——你应该能像翻素材柜一样浏览自己工程里所有的模型、贴图、场景、角色,而不是在文件夹里盲找。"拖拽到场景"则要求把这条路径做到顺滑自然:从浏览器里抓起一个模型,拖到 3D 视图里松手,它就以合理的位置、朝向、尺寸落进场景,并正确接好材质与引用——而不是丢进去一团错位或没贴图的东西。把这两件事按 UE5 这种被千万开发者验证过的范式来对标,意味着目标不是"能用就行",而是"用起来和专业引擎一样顺手"。这一天交付的是这条路的完整设计与对标清单,为随后的真正落地铺好了图纸。The design targets the two most frequent acts in creation: finding an asset and placing it into the world. The "content browser" is benchmarked against the categorized, thumbnailed, searchable asset library of a mature engine — you should be able to browse every model, texture, scene and character in your project like flipping through a material cabinet, instead of hunting blindly in folders. "Drag-to-scene" then demands that path feel smooth and natural: grab a model from the browser, drop it into the 3D view, and it lands in the scene at a sensible position, orientation and size with its materials and references correctly wired — not a misplaced or untextured blob. Benchmarking both against a paradigm proven by millions of developers, like UE5's, means the goal isn't "good enough to work" but "as comfortable as a professional engine." What this day delivered is the full design and parity checklist for that path, laying the blueprint for the real implementation that follows.

编辑器有了自己的质检门The editor gets its own QA gate

另一件事关乎可靠性。可视化编辑器这块,已经独立成一个能单独构建、单独运行的部分;这一天进一步给它装上了一道属于它自己的自动质检门:每次改动都要先经过一套自动化的构建与检查,通过了才算数。这听起来不起眼,却是任何一个会持续长大的部件能保持健康的关键。编辑器以后会不断加面板、加交互、加能力;没有这道门,某个改动可能在不被注意的角落悄悄弄坏了另一处,直到用户撞上才发现。有了这道门,问题会在最早、最便宜的阶段——也就是改动刚发生时——就被拦下,而不是流到你手里。换句话说,这一天给编辑器装的不是新功能,而是"长大的过程中不退步"的保障。The other thing is about reliability. The visual editor has already become a part that builds and runs on its own; this day went further and gave it its own automated quality gate: every change must first pass a suite of automated build-and-check steps before it counts. It sounds unremarkable, yet it's the key to keeping any continuously growing component healthy. The editor will keep gaining panels, interactions and capabilities; without this gate, some change could quietly break another corner unnoticed until a user hits it. With it, problems are caught at the earliest and cheapest stage — the moment the change is made — instead of flowing to you. In other words, what this day added to the editor isn't a new feature but a guarantee of "not regressing as it grows."

这一天意味着什么What this day means

不是每一天都该是新功能的烟花。一个想做得长久、做得专业的工具,需要在冲刺之间穿插这样的"地基日":把方向想清楚、把标准定下来、把质量护栏架好。这一天做的两件事正是如此——用一份对标 UE5 的设计,给"资产怎么用"这条最高频的路径定了专业级的目标;用一道自动质检门,给正在快速长大的编辑器上了保险。它们都不会立刻在画面上变出什么,但它们决定了接下来真正动手时,能不能做得又快又稳、又不踩坑。把目标定高、把护栏架牢,后面的每一步才走得踏实。Not every day should be a fireworks of new features. A tool that means to last and to be professional needs "foundation days" interleaved between the sprints: getting the direction clear, setting the standard, building the quality guardrails. The two things this day did are exactly that — a UE5-grade design setting a professional target for the most frequent path of "how assets get used," and an automated QA gate insuring the fast-growing editor. Neither conjures anything on screen immediately, but together they decide whether the real building that follows can be fast, steady and pitfall-free. Aim high, rail it tight, and every later step lands on solid ground.
