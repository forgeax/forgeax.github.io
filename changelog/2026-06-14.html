特效不再"全屏白爆":火球终于稳稳是红的No more screen-wide white-out: the fireball finally stays red

一个折腾了一整天的视觉问题被彻底根治。此前的现象很夸张:在那款弹幕生存游戏里一放火球、一开火、一爆炸,整个屏幕就被冲成一块大白板,角色和场景全淹没在白光里,根本没法玩。难就难在它不是单一原因,而是好几种效果叠加共振的结果:辉光(让亮的地方发光晕)开得太强、粒子用了一种"越叠越亮"的混合方式、物体的自发光被拉到了顶、子弹的拖尾又在屏幕上一层层往上加——任何一个单独看都还好,凑在一起就互相放大,最终把整个画面推到纯白。这天的修复是靠真实截图一步步逼出真凶,然后对症逐项处理:调低辉光、把粒子和特效换成一种"叠加不会失真"的混合方式、把过度的自发光压下来、子弹改用正常的透明混合而不是越叠越亮、并干脆去掉那条会在屏幕上糊成一片白的拖尾。最后还顺手补回了地面的投影、把方向光调斜让阴影看得见,画面的明暗层次重新清楚。对所有想用本平台做带华丽特效游戏的人来说,这是把一类极常见、又极难自己定位的坑,从根上填平了。A visual problem that took a whole day to chase was fixed at the root. The symptom was extreme: in that bullet-survival game, firing a fireball, shooting or exploding washed the whole screen into a big white board, drowning character and scene in white light, unplayable. The difficulty was that it had no single cause but resulted from several effects stacking and resonating: bloom (which makes bright areas glow) set too strong, particles using a blend mode that "only gets brighter as it layers," objects' emissive maxed out, and bullet trails piling up layer on layer on the screen — each fine alone, but together they amplify one another and push the whole image to pure white. This day's fix chased down the culprits step by step using real screenshots, then addressed each: lowering bloom, switching particles and effects to a blend mode that "doesn't distort when layered," dampening excessive emissive, changing bullets to normal transparent blending instead of ever-brighter, and outright removing the trail that smeared into white across the screen. It then restored ground shadows and tilted the directional light to make them visible, so light-and-dark gradation reads clearly again. For anyone wanting to build effect-rich games on the platform, this fills in, at the root, a very common and very hard-to-self-diagnose class of pitfall.

导入的场景能正常走、跳、上台阶Imported scenes are walkable, with jumping and step-up

一个导入进来的仓库场景这天从"摆设"变成了"能玩"。此前的问题有两个:一是它根本没有碰撞,角色一进去就直接掉穿地面,像幽灵一样落进虚空;二是它的比例错得离谱,整个仓库被缩小了一大截,显得角色像个钻不进门的巨人。这天先修正了真实比例,让仓库的大小和角色对得上;再做了一套逐节点的碰撞——场景里的每一个网格部件(墙、地、箱子、台阶等)都各自算出一个包围盒,系统据此一次性推导出"哪里是能站、能走的地面"。在此之上又加了跳跃和"自动上台阶":遇到不太高的台阶或边沿,角色会自动抬脚踏上去,而不是被卡住。现在你能在这个仓库里跑动、跳跃、踩着台阶往上走,把它当成一张真正的关卡来玩。这件事的意义,是把前几天打通的"导入外部模型"这条路又往前接了一段:模型不仅能看、能摆,现在还能真正"踩上去玩"——从一个静态的展示物,变成了一个有物理、能交互的游戏空间。An imported warehouse scene went from "scenery" to "playable" this day. There were two prior problems: one, it had no collision at all, so a character entering dropped straight through the floor like a ghost into the void; two, its scale was wildly off, the whole warehouse shrunk way down so the character looked like a giant who couldn't fit through a door. This day first corrected the real scale so the warehouse and character match; then built per-node collision — each mesh part in the scene (walls, floor, crates, stairs and so on) computes its own bounding box, from which the system derives all at once "where you can stand and walk." On top of that, jumping and automatic step-up were added: facing a not-too-tall step or ledge, the character automatically lifts a foot onto it instead of getting stuck. Now you can run, jump and climb stairs inside this warehouse and play it as a real level. The significance is extending the "import external models" road opened over the preceding days another stretch: a model isn't just viewable and placeable but now actually "steppable and playable" — going from a static showpiece to a game space with physics and interaction.

3D 生成工作台:会评分、会调参、能多角度看The 3D-generation workbench: scores, tunes and views from any angle

3D 生成工作台这天从"会出模型"升级到了"会帮你判断好坏、会让你调"。它一天里上了三个大功能。其一是更专业的预览器:支持切换环境、阴影、线框等多种显示模式,你能从不同光照、不同角度去审视一个生成出来的模型,而不再只能盯着一个固定视图,模型的细节和问题都更容易看清。其二是五维质量评分:系统会自动从网格、贴图、拓扑、动画、整体这五个维度给生成结果打分,把"这个模型到底好不好"从主观感觉变成了一眼可见的具体指标——尤其当你一次生成好几个候选时,评分能帮你快速挑出最好的那个。其三是透传各家生成服务的进阶参数:以前你只能用默认配置生成,现在能调出不同服务各自的高级选项,针对性地控制生成效果。这三件事合起来,把工作台从一个"输入文字、输出模型"的单向黑盒,变成了一条"生成 → 评估 → 调参 → 再生成"的可迭代闭环——你不再是被动接受 AI 给你什么,而是能看着评分、调着参数,主动把结果逼近你想要的样子。The 3D-generation workbench went from "produces a model" to "helps you judge it and lets you tune it" this day, shipping three big features in a day. First, a more professional previewer: supporting toggles for environment, shadows, wireframe and more display modes, so you can examine a generated model under different lighting and angles rather than one fixed view, making its details and problems easier to see. Second, five-dimension quality scoring: the system automatically rates a result on mesh, texture, topology, animation and overall, turning "is this model any good" from a subjective feeling into visible concrete metrics — especially when you generate several candidates at once, the score helps you quickly pick the best. Third, exposing the advanced parameters of each generation service: where you previously could only generate with defaults, you can now bring up each service's advanced options to control the result deliberately. Together these turn the workbench from a one-way black box of "type text, get a model" into an iterable loop of "generate → evaluate → tune → regenerate" — you're no longer passively accepting whatever the AI gives, but can watch the score and adjust the parameters to actively drive the result toward what you want.

对话与 agent 更不容易卡Chat and agents are harder to get stuck

这天后端和聊天又收掉了一批容易卡顿的环节,专攻"AI 在干活途中会不会突然僵住"这类问题。后端方面:当你中断一个 agent 向你发起的"询问"时,界面现在永远不会再被卡住——以前这种中断偶尔会让整个界面僵在那里;同时给模型输出加了边界保护,当输出过长或溢出时能稳妥处理而不是直接崩。前端方面:给流式输出(模型一个字一个字往外吐的过程)做了节流,避免刷新太频繁拖累界面;加了一个命令授权的界面,让需要你确认的敏感操作有了清楚的交互;还修掉了那个询问卡片偶尔会卡在"正在准备选项"、迟迟出不来选项的问题。这些改动单看都很细碎,但它们共同针对的是同一种最让人焦虑的体验——"它是不是死了?"。一个让你把任务长时间交给它自己跑的 AI,绝不能动不动就僵在那里让你不知所措;把这些会卡住的地方一个个堵上,正是让人能安心把后台交给它的前提。This day the backend and chat closed another batch of stall-prone links, focusing on the class of problem "does the AI suddenly freeze mid-work." On the backend: when you abort an "ask" an agent raised to you, the interface now never freezes again — such an abort used to occasionally leave the whole interface stuck; and guards were added for model output, handling over-long or overflowing output gracefully rather than crashing. On the front end: streaming output (the model spilling text character by character) was throttled to avoid refreshing so often it drags the interface; a command-authorization UI was added, giving sensitive operations that need your confirmation a clear interaction; and the bug where the ask card occasionally got stuck on "preparing options" and was slow to show them was fixed. Individually fine-grained, these together target the same most anxiety-inducing experience — "is it dead?" An AI you hand tasks to run on its own for long stretches must never keep freezing and leaving you at a loss; plugging these stall points one by one is exactly the precondition for confidently handing it the background.

Curing the fireball white-out is making "visual expression," already doable, genuinely good-looking; making imported scenes walkable extends the already-opened "import models" road into "playable"; the workbench learning to score and tune upgrades "can generate" into "can use well"; and the conversation loop's stall-resistance polishes "usable" into "solid in use."
