特效不再白爆 + 导入场景能走能跳Effects stop washing white + walk-and-jump in imported scenes

特效终于保住自己的颜色、不再冲成全屏白;导入的场景能正常行走、跳跃、上台阶;3D 生成工作台会评分、会调参、能多角度看。Effects finally keep their color instead of blowing the screen white; imported scenes are now walkable with jumping and step-up; the 3D-generation workbench gains scoring, tuning and multi-angle viewing.

特效不再"全屏白爆",火球终于是红的No more screen-wide white-out — the fireball is finally red

一个折腾了一整天的视觉问题被根治:之前一放火球 / 子弹 / 爆炸,整个屏幕会被冲成大白板,连角色都看不见。根因是多种效果叠加——辉光太强、粒子用了会越叠越亮的混合方式、自发光拉满、子弹拖尾又在屏幕上层层叠加。这天逐一拆解修复后,特效能稳稳保住自己的颜色:火球是红的就一直是红的,而不是越烧越白。地面阴影也补了回来,画面层次清楚了。对所有用本平台做带特效游戏的人,这是一类常见坑的根治。A visual problem that took a whole day to chase was fixed at the root: firing a fireball, bullet or explosion used to wash the entire screen to a white board, hiding even the character. The cause was several effects stacking — bloom too strong, particles using a blend mode that only gets brighter as it layers, emissive maxed out, and bullet trails piling up on top of the screen. After taking them apart one by one, effects now hold their color steadily: a red fireball stays red instead of burning whiter and whiter. Ground shadows came back too, so the image reads clearly. For anyone building effect-heavy games on the platform, this fixes a common class of pitfall.

导入的场景能正常走、跳、上台阶Imported scenes are walkable, with jumping and step-up

一个导入进来的仓库场景从"摆设"变成了"能玩":之前它没有碰撞、比例还错了一大截(角色像巨人钻不进门),一进去直接掉穿地面。这天修正了真实比例,并做了一套逐节点的碰撞——场景里每个网格部件各有自己的包围盒,系统一次性算出可行走的地面;再加上跳跃和"自动上台阶",你现在能在场景里跑动、跳跃、踏着台阶往上走。把外部模型当真实关卡来玩,这条路打通了。An imported warehouse scene went from "scenery" to "playable": it previously had no collision and the wrong scale (the character was giant-sized and couldn't fit through doors), so stepping in dropped you straight through the floor. This day corrected the real scale and built per-node collision — each mesh part in the scene has its own bounding box, and the system computes the walkable ground from them all at once; with jumping and automatic step-up added, you can now run, jump and climb stairs inside the scene. Playing an external model as a real level now works.

3D 生成工作台:会评分、会调参、能多角度看The 3D-generation workbench: scores, tunes and views from any angle

3D 生成工作台从"会出模型"升级到"会帮你判断好坏、会让你调"。新的预览器支持切换环境、阴影、线框等显示模式,看模型不再只能盯一个角度;还加了五维质量评分,自动从网格、贴图、拓扑、动画、整体五个角度给生成结果打分,好不好一眼可见;并把各家生成服务的进阶参数透传出来,不再只能跑默认配置。生成 → 评估 → 调参再生成,形成了一条闭环。The 3D-generation workbench went from "produces a model" to "helps you judge it and lets you tune it." A new previewer supports toggling environment, shadows and wireframe display modes, so you're no longer stuck looking from one angle; a five-dimension quality score was added, automatically rating a result on mesh, texture, topology, animation and overall, so quality is visible at a glance; and the advanced parameters of each generation service are now exposed, so you're not limited to the defaults. Generate → evaluate → tune and regenerate now forms a loop.

对话与 agent 更不容易卡Chat and agents are harder to get stuck

后端和聊天又收掉一批卡顿:中断一个"询问"时界面永不再被卡住,对超长 / 溢出的模型输出加了边界保护;前端给流式输出做了节流、加了命令授权界面,并修掉了询问卡片偶尔停在"正在准备选项"的问题。长时间使用更顺、更可控。The backend and chat closed another batch of stalls: aborting an "ask" never freezes the interface again, and guards were added for over-long or overflowing model output; the front end throttled streaming output, added a command-authorization UI, and fixed the ask card occasionally getting stuck on "preparing options." Long sessions feel smoother and more in control.
