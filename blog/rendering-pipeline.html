渲染管线:PBR、IBL 与 SSAORendering: PBR, IBL & SSAO

游戏好不好看,渲染管线说了算。ForgeaX 的自研渲染链已经覆盖了现代实时渲染的主要环节 —— 而且 AI 生成的游戏开箱就能用上。How good a game looks comes down to the render pipeline. ForgeaX's self-built rendering chain now covers the main stages of modern real-time rendering — and AI-generated games get it out of the box.

已经具备的能力What's in

  • PBR 材质 —— 基于物理的着色,金属/粗糙度工作流,光照表现自然。PBR materials — physically-based shading with a metalness/roughness workflow for natural lighting.
  • IBL + HDR 天空盒 —— 基于图像的环境光照,场景从环境贴图里"接"到真实的间接光与反射。IBL + HDR skybox — image-based lighting, so scenes pick up believable indirect light and reflections from an environment map.
  • SSAO —— 屏幕空间环境光遮蔽,给角落、接缝加上柔和阴影,画面立刻有体积感。SSAO — screen-space ambient occlusion adds soft contact shadows in corners and seams, giving scenes instant depth.
  • 抗锯齿 —— MSAA 与 FXAA,边缘干净不毛糙。Anti-aliasing — MSAA and FXAA for clean, un-jaggy edges.
  • 后处理链 + render-graph —— 自研材质链与 WGSL 着色器库,按 render-graph 组织,易扩展。Post-processing + render-graph — a self-built material chain and WGSL shader library, organized as a render-graph that's easy to extend.

跑在双 RHI 之上On top of dual RHI

这套管线建立在两套渲染后端(WebGPU 与一套 wasm 实现)的统一接口之上。两套实现走同一套 API、同时出图,既保证一致性,也在 AI 写错渲染代码时互相验证。The pipeline sits on a unified interface over two rendering backends (WebGPU and a wasm implementation). Both run the same API and render side by side — ensuring consistency and cross-checking each other when AI writes rendering code.

对你意味着什么What it means for you

你不需要懂任何这些术语。当你描述"一个黄昏氛围的场景"或"金属质感的机甲",引擎已经具备把它渲染好看的底子 —— AI 调用的是同一套管线。You don't need to know any of these terms. When you describe "a dusk-lit scene" or "a metallic mech," the engine already has what it takes to render it well — AI calls the very same pipeline.
